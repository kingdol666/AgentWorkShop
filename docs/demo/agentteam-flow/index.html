AgentTeam Closed-Loop Optimization on a Simulated Production Line

End-to-end demonstration of the AgentWorkShop supervisory layer: five fieldbus PLCs (cast-film extrusion twin, 6 writable setpoints + 5 process quantities) are commissioned on the platform, an AgentTeam is assembled and deployed, an optimization goal is dispatched from the team composer, and a governed closed loop drives film thickness into the 50 ± 2 μm target window — every write interlocked, attributed and journalled.

What the PLC simulator is

A cast-film extrusion twin (deterministic, seeded physics engine): three zone-temperature setpoints, screw speed, line speed and die gap act on first-order thermal dynamics and mass-conservation thickness — observed through melt temperature, melt pressure, film thickness, defect rate and gels count.

How the team connects

The worker is bound to nodes; bindings surface semantic cards (quantity, unit, safe range, active recipe window) through my_industrial_nodes, and open exactly two capabilities: daq_query (read) and dcw_control (write, interlocked).

Why the writes are trustworthy

Each write is checked against the recipe window, holds a read-back verification, opens an optimization record, is journalled with agent attribution, and is judged (keep / rollback) against acquisition evidence.
