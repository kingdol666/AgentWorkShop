7 · Method & environment

  • Executed strictly per the bench/PIPELINE.md §0 execution card. Cold boot on an isolated config root (AW_HOME): the pipeline auto-started platform and PLC simulator; first-boot admin self-registration; resident instances on other ports were never touched (no single-instance contention).
