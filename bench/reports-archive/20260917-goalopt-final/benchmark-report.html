7 · Method & environment

  • Executed strictly per the bench/PIPELINE.md §0 execution card. Cold boot: the pipeline auto-started platform and PLC simulator from a wiped data root (first-boot admin self-registration).
  • Determinism: all randomness from --seed 42 (mulberry32); fixture tags randomized per run; every run lands in bench/results/<runId>, never overwritten.
