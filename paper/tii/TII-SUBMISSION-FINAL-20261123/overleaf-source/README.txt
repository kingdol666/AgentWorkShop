Overleaf source package: TII initial-submission manuscript, 2026-11-23

1. Upload the ZIP as a new Overleaf project; main.tex is at the archive root.
2. Select main.tex and pdfLaTeX. Bibliography uses BibTeX (IEEEtran style).
3. Figures 2 and 3 use PNG images; Figures 1, 4, 5, and 6 use vector PDFs.
4. The anonymized manuscript is self-contained; no supplementary files are
   required to compile or review it. Compiling this package reproduces the
   delivered PDF: 10 pages, US Letter, all fonts embedded.
5. Authors should verify declarations and current journal submission
   requirements.
