**Working status: 2026-11-07.** This is an author-only disclosure draft. The
anonymous-review PDF omits the Acknowledgment section; the destination for a

## Draft disclosure for author confirmation

> OpenAI ChatGPT and Codex assisted with restructuring, drafting, and language
> revision across Sections I--VII, and with code used to prepare Figures 1, 4,
> and 5. OpenAI Images 2.6 was requested through Codex's image-generation
> tool to generate the cast-film line illustration used as the background in
> Figure 1 (Section I); the tool did not expose an API subvariant or build
> identifier. Its labels, signal mappings, and connectors were composed
> separately in code and checked against the system description.
> OpenAI GPT Image, accessed through the Codex image-generation tool (the exact
> model build was not exposed), was also used to enhance the user-supplied
> raster diagrams used as Figures 2 and 3 (Sections III and IV), focusing on
> legibility and color contrast while retaining their labels and flow. The
> Real-ESRGAN x4plus-anime model was then used for 4x raster super-resolution.
> The authors checked labels, arrows, and figure semantics against the paper
> and system description. No AI system generated experimental data. The
> authors reviewed and remain responsible for the manuscript, figures, code,
> and claims.

The author team must confirm that this wording covers the actual tools and
assistance used. The image-generation tool did not expose an API subvariant or
model-build identifier; do not infer one from the file date. Confirm the final
disclosure wording and its destination in the submission system.

IEEE's public guideline requires disclosure of AI-generated content used in an
article, including figures, images, and code, and asks authors to identify the
system, affected sections, and extent of use. Grammar-only assistance is
treated separately. See the [IEEE AI-generated content
guideline](https://open.ieee.org/author-guidelines-for-artificial-intelligence-ai-generated-text/).
