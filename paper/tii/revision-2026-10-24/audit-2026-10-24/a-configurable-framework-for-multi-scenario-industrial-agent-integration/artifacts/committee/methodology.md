## Methodology Transparency Review (SRQR-aware)

Scores: Soundness 8.0/10; Reproducibility 7.2/10.

Review basis: the pre-edit manuscript snapshot (`main.tex`, `sections/evaluation.tex`, `sections/discussion.tex`, `REPRODUCIBILITY.md`) and the then-current 10-page `main.pdf`. This is an evidence-scope and reproducibility review, not a review of the prose edits now in progress.

### MUST-FIX (submission blockers)
- None for the claims as bounded in the reviewed snapshot. The paper explicitly says Q1–Q6 are separate archives and does not report a paired strategy comparison, physical-plant validation, or broad transfer result.

### SHOULD-FIX (quality improvements)
- **Cross-archive scope (moderate; evidence limitation, not a numerical error).** “The archives are complementary implementation evidence, not one joint current-version trial” (Experimental Evaluation, lines 10–13). The boundary is accurate and is repeated in Engineering Interpretation. Q1–Q4 are historical direct-node traces; Q5 is a software-assertion campaign; Q6 is one accelerated, scripted-approval recipe trajectory. Thus, the evidence supports separately exercised configuration and route behavior across the named archives, but does not show the current agent harness, profile/capability selection, and process outcome operating together across multiple scenes. Keep this limit adjacent to any headline multi-scenario result. A stronger current-version end-to-end claim requires a new matched run; wording alone cannot supply that evidence.
- **Exact R1 replayability (moderate; reproducibility limitation).** The companion notes say, “A source commit recorded in a run does not prove that every uncommitted harness file matched that commit” and report an 11-file R1 harness fingerprint versus nine files in the recorded commit (Reproducibility Notes, lines 29–40). The archived outputs and raw records are available and hash-identified, but the exact historical runtime worktree is not; an independent reader can audit the recorded outcomes but cannot fully rerun that exact Q1–Q4 harness. Preserve this distinction between archive auditability and exact replayability. If the missing worktree cannot be recovered, do not imply exact rerun reproducibility.
- **Claim-preserving archive-ID polish (minor).** In the reviewed snapshot, Q1–Q4 and the ablation name raw run-folder IDs in the procedure, whereas Q5 is dated and Q6 uses its R6 alias (Experimental Evaluation, lines 18–26 and 263–265). The companion evidence map already assigns stable IDs R1–R12 and exact paths (Reproducibility Notes, lines 44–57). If raw IDs are removed from the body, retain the short R1/R2/R3/R6 links in the relevant experiment descriptions and keep full paths in the anonymous evidence package. This improves traceability without changing a result or claim.
- **Do not inflate evidence strength during polishing.** Q4 is one seed-42 trajectory per process; Q6 is one sequential trajectory; Q5's 782 checks are deterministic software assertions across nine sequential executions, not 782 plant samples. Do not add confidence bands or inferential language to these observations. Q3's (J/J^*) is normalized to a finite admissible grid and is not a global optimum; keep the present qualification.

### SRQR Checklist Deltas
- Sampling rationale: SRQR sampling is not applicable. For the quantitative evidence units, the paper states one seed-42 trajectory per Q4 process, three seeds for Q3, and one Q6 trajectory; these support observed route behavior, not population-level robustness.
- Data collection details (time/place/duration): The evidence units, simulator setting, seed counts, protocol coverage, write counts, wait intervals, approval mode, and Q6 pacing overrides are identified. Q6's absent melt-temperature observation and limited freshness check are disclosed (Evaluation, lines 272–286 and 321–324).
- Coding process (stages, coders, disagreement resolution): Not applicable; no qualitative coding study is reported.
- Saturation: Not applicable; this is not a qualitative sampling study.
- Triangulation: The archives are deliberately kept separate. Q6 pairs trial receipts with platform-node/DAQ snapshots; the paper also explicitly says its three-way endpoint agreement is not process-convergence evidence. Do not describe separate archives as repeated measurements of one current-version trial.
- Reflexivity: Not applicable; no human-subject interviews or qualitative interpretation are reported. Q6 approvals are scripted and this is stated.
