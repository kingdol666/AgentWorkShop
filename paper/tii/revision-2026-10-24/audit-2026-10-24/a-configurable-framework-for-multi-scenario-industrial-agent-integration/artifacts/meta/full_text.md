A Configurable Framework for Multi-Scenario Industrial Agent Integration

Anonymous Authors
IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics, Submitted ManuscriptConfigurable Industrial Agent Integration



abstract
Industrial-agent systems must connect reasoning and process analysis to
production context and heterogeneous equipment, yet each scene brings
different signals, protocols, targets, and operating rules. This paper
presents a configurable framework that combines agent teams
and configurable agent-engine adapters with production context, analysis and modeling services,
semantic acquisition and write control, and route-specific workflows. Tasks
are scoped to line, product, recipe, and run. Process evidence informs a
candidate; channel profiles and capabilities select an eligible node or
recipe route. Each route preserves distinct command, observation, and
production-record steps. Evaluation draws on separate archives: four
simulator scenes met their terminal checks; acquisition covered five protocol
families, while writes used four protocol mappings; nine deployment-suite runs
passed 782 assertions. Two same-setup live-engine tasks and one deterministic
controller across three seeds further exercised proposal and command paths.
A separate accelerated recipe trajectory recorded four approved trial writes
from 45 to 65 bar and weight from 31.28 to 32.30 g; the accepted recipe
version was saved but not applied. These results support configuration-based
integration in the tested simulator settings. They do not establish physical
transfer, broad plant coverage, or comparative control benefit.
