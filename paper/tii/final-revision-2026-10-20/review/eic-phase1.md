## Contract Paraphrase

D1 requires the study design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility provisions to meet the field's peer-review standard, with methodological rigor assessed by the methodology seat.

D2 requires the manuscript's claims to agree with current domain evidence, represent prior work accurately, and use domain terminology and reported results without factual errors, with domain accuracy assessed by the domain seat.

D3 requires an internally consistent thesis whose evidence supports its claims without fallacies that undermine the central argument, with argumentative coherence owned by the devil's-advocate seat and eligible methodology input.

D4 requires framing, definitions, and implications that adjacent-field readers can understand, with interdisciplinary claims supported sufficiently for cross-disciplinary relevance, assessed by the perspective seat.

D5 requires clear manuscript organization and exposition, usable figures and tables, and adherence to applicable venue conventions, with writing and structure assessed by the EIC seat.

D6 requires a fit between the manuscript, the configured venue, and its readership, together with an original and significant contribution appropriate to that venue, with venue fit and contribution assessed by the EIC seat.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Clear organization, readable exposition, effective figures and tables, and adherence to applicable venue conventions.
what_triggers_block: Structure or presentation is so disordered or noncompliant that readers cannot reliably follow the work or evaluate it.
what_triggers_warn: Noticeable clarity, organization, visual-quality, or venue-convention problems materially impede communication but remain repairable.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Alignment with the configured venue's scope and readership, together with an original and sufficiently significant contribution.
what_triggers_block: Venue alignment or contribution is too limited or derivative for the configured readership and requires substantial repositioning or rework.
what_triggers_warn: Venue fit or contribution appears plausible but is incompletely established, modest, or in need of clearer framing.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript is fundamentally outside the configured venue or lacks any original contribution, making acceptance impossible even after ordinary revision.
