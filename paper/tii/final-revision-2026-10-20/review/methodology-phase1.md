## Contract Paraphrase

D1 methodology_rigor requires the study design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility provisions to meet the field’s peer-review standard. From the methodology seat, this means checking whether the methods can answer the stated questions, whether the data and analyses are handled transparently and appropriately, and whether another researcher could understand and repeat the main work.

D2 domain_accuracy requires the manuscript’s claims and terminology to align with current domain evidence and to represent prior work and reported results accurately. Although domain fact-checking belongs primarily to another seat, the methodology commitment recognizes that methodological conclusions must not depend on mischaracterized evidence, technical definitions, or reported outcomes.

D3 argumentative_coherence requires the central thesis to remain internally consistent, for the evidence to support the claims made from it, and for no reasoning error to undermine the main argument. The methodology review therefore checks whether the inferential chain from question through design, analysis, results, and conclusion is logically connected and appropriately bounded.

D4 cross_disciplinary_relevance requires framing, definitions, and implications to be understandable to adjacent-field readers and requires interdisciplinary claims to have supporting justification. From a methods perspective, this includes whether constructs, assumptions, and limits are stated clearly enough that readers outside the immediate specialty can evaluate the basis of the claims.

D5 writing_and_structure requires organization, exposition, figures and tables, and venue conventions to support clear evaluation of the work. The methodology seat treats presentation as relevant where unclear structure, incomplete methodological disclosure, or unreadable result displays obstructs verification of the design or evidence.

D6 venue_fit_and_contribution requires an appropriate match to the configured venue and an original, significant contribution for its readership. The methodology seat does not decide novelty or editorial fit, but it recognizes that contribution claims must be proportionate to the design, evidence quality, and reproducible support actually provided.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor
dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Alignment between research questions and design; transparent data provenance and handling; appropriate measurements and analyses; reported assumptions, uncertainty, effect sizes, and limitations; complete results; and sufficient procedural, data, and code detail for reproducibility.
what_triggers_block: A substantial but repairable design, data-handling, analysis, or reproducibility deficiency prevents reliable evaluation of whether the methods support the main claims or prevents replication of the primary analysis without a major methodological revision.
what_triggers_warn: Localized omissions or underdeveloped checks, such as incomplete assumption reporting, limited sensitivity analysis, missing effect-size or uncertainty context, or partial reproducibility detail, reduce confidence while leaving the core design and inference evaluable.
what_triggers_fatal: The central design is fundamentally incapable of answering the stated question, the data or analysis basis is irreparably invalid or unavailable, or an essential methodological failure makes the principal inference impossible to rescue through revision.

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: A consistent thesis; explicit links from questions to evidence and conclusions; claims calibrated to the design and results; treatment of alternative explanations and boundary conditions; and absence of inferential leaps or fallacies that undermine the core argument.
what_triggers_block: A major but repairable break in the evidence-to-claim chain, unresolved contradiction, or overreach requires substantive restructuring or qualification before the central argument can be assessed reliably.
what_triggers_warn: A bounded gap, ambiguity, or localized overstatement weakens argumentative clarity or claim calibration but does not overturn the central line of reasoning and can be corrected through targeted clarification.
what_triggers_fatal: The central thesis depends on mutually inconsistent premises, unsupported evidence, or an invalid inferential structure such that the main conclusion cannot follow even after ordinary methodological repair.
