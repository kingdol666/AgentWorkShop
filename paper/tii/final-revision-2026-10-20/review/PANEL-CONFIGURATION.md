# Five-seat role-separated review

Configuration previously accepted by the author and retained for this revision.

- EIC: systems contribution, structure, narrative, and figure communication.
- Methodology: experimental units, reproducibility, measurement semantics, arithmetic, and evidence scope.
- Domain: industrial agents, semantic I/O, authorization, production-context design, and implementation fidelity.
- Perspective: deployment responsibility, human interaction, cross-disciplinary readability, and practical use.
- Devil's Advocate: strongest counterargument, alternative explanations, and core claim validity.

Each seat uses a fresh invocation context with no inherited paper content, commits its yardstick before seeing the manuscript, then reads the frozen manuscript in a second call. Peers' reports are withheld until commitment. All seats use the primary model family; role/context separation does not imply statistically independent errors. Accountable human identity is not supplied. No external-provider upload is used.

The canonical conformance, panel synthesis and provenance scripts were not found in the installed vendor/skill directories. Local structural checks will be labelled as such, and cannot be reported as canonical conformance. No resolved ReviewCriteriaBindingManifest is supplied: `criteria_binding_unavailable`. Scientific judgements are qualitative and `NOT_CALIBRATED`; official IEEE formatting checks are a separate source-backed assessment.
