## Contract Paraphrase

D1 sets the bar for whether a study's design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility details can be evaluated rigorously and understood by readers beyond a narrow specialty.

D2 requires claims and terminology to match the relevant domain evidence and prior work, including accurate representation of reported results.

D3 asks whether the central thesis remains internally consistent, whether the evidence supports its claims, and whether reasoning gaps undermine the argument.

D4 concerns whether the framing and definitions are accessible to adjacent-field readers and whether interdisciplinary claims and implications have adequate support.

D5 covers whether the manuscript's organization, exposition, visual materials, and presentation conform to the relevant venue's expectations.

D6 asks whether the work is appropriate for the configured venue's readership and presents an original, sufficiently significant contribution for that audience.

## Scoring Plan

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance

dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Assess whether framing and key terms are understandable to adjacent-discipline readers, and whether interdisciplinary claims and implications have clear supporting rationale and evidence.
what_triggers_block: A central framing or interdisciplinary implication is so undefined or unsupported that an adjacent-field reader cannot determine what relevance is being claimed or assess its basis.
what_triggers_warn: A noncentral term, framing choice, or implication would benefit from added context or support, while adjacent-field readers can still follow and assess the central relevance.
