## Contract Paraphrase

D1 methodology_rigor requires study design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility support to meet the field's peer-review standard; this seat records the contract dimension but does not score methodology.

D2 domain_accuracy requires the manuscript's substantive claims to agree with current domain evidence, to represent prior work correctly, and to use domain-specific terminology and reported results without factual error; this is the sole dimension assigned to the domain seat.

D3 argumentative_coherence requires an internally consistent thesis whose evidence supports its claims and whose reasoning avoids fallacies that would undermine the central argument; this seat records the dimension but does not assess it.

D4 cross_disciplinary_relevance requires framing, definitions, and implications to remain understandable to adjacent-field readers and requires interdisciplinary claims to have adequate support; this seat records the dimension but does not assess it.

D5 writing_and_structure requires clear organisation and exposition, usable figures and tables, and compliance with venue conventions; this seat records the dimension but does not assess it.

D6 venue_fit_and_contribution requires the manuscript to suit the configured venue and to make an original and significant contribution appropriate to that venue's readership; this seat records the dimension but does not assess it.

## Scoring Plan

### D2: domain_accuracy
dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: Check whether substantive claims align with current domain evidence, prior work is represented faithfully, and domain-specific terminology and reported results are factually precise.
what_triggers_block: Substantive domain errors or materially distorted prior-work representations undermine the central claims and require major correction before acceptance.
what_triggers_warn: Localized terminology imprecision, incomplete domain context, or limited literature coverage weakens confidence without overturning the core claims.
what_triggers_fatal: A central domain claim is demonstrably false, or foundational prior work is materially misrepresented such that the core contribution is invalid without a fundamental rebuild.
