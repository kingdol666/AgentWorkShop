## Contract Paraphrase

D1 methodology_rigor requires the study design, data treatment, statistical presentation, and reproducibility support to meet the applicable peer-review standard.

D2 domain_accuracy requires the manuscript's claims to match current domain evidence, represent prior work accurately, and use domain-specific terms and results without factual error.

D3 argumentative_coherence requires the central thesis to remain internally consistent, with evidence that supports the claims and no fallacies that undermine the main argument.

D4 cross_disciplinary_relevance requires framing and definitions to be understandable to adjacent-field readers and requires interdisciplinary implications to be supported rather than asserted.

D5 writing_and_structure requires clear organisation, understandable exposition, usable figures and tables, and compliance with the configured venue's presentation conventions.

D6 venue_fit_and_contribution requires the work to fit the configured venue and to offer an original and sufficiently significant contribution for that venue's readership.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the central thesis remains internally consistent and each material claim is supported by the evidence presented, without reasoning errors that undermine the argument.
what_triggers_block: A substantive contradiction or unsupported inferential leap in the core thesis means the argument cannot be accepted without major repair.
what_triggers_warn: A material but repairable gap, ambiguity, or limited mismatch between evidence and an important claim weakens coherence while leaving the thesis potentially recoverable.
what_triggers_fatal: The central argument is invalidated by a decisive contradiction, fallacy, or absence of necessary support that cannot be resolved through ordinary revision.
