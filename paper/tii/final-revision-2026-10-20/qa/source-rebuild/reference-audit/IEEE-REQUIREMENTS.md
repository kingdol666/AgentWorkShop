# Official requirements and project acceptance criteria

Checked on 2026-10-20 against the live IEEE IES and IEEE Author Center pages. Extracted source text and retrieval dates are saved in `qa/official-requirements-snapshots.json`. Web tool requests returned empty results; public official pages were retrieved directly over HTTPS. No manuscript or bibliography metadata was uploaded.

## Official journal rules

- TII regular-research new submissions have a strict ten-page maximum, applicable to new submissions since 2025-01-01. Accepted versions have a separate twelve-page ceiling. Exactly ten pages is the author's delivery preference, not a journal minimum.
- Review manuscripts must use IEEE double-column format and be anonymous: no author names, affiliations, biographies, identifying footnotes, or acknowledgments. PDF limit: 40 MB. Fonts must be embedded or portable.
- The manuscript must clearly explain its original contribution for qualified readers. References must be discussed in the text and include recent relevant work. No universal thirty-reference ceiling was found.

## Official graphics guidance

- Vector PS/EPS/PDF is preferred for resizable diagrams. PDF is an accepted submission-artwork format; SVG is retained here as editable source.
- Standard widths are 3.5 in (single column) and 7.16 in (double column). Type should appear approximately 9–10 pt at full size; Arial is among IEEE's recommended fonts, and PDF fonts should be embedded or outlined.
- Non-vector color/grayscale images require >300 dpi; black-and-white line art requires >600 dpi. Vector submission diagrams are evaluated by their actual geometry/font readability rather than raster DPI. A 600-dpi preview is not the submission artwork.
- The inspected pages do not prescribe a single TII color palette, border width, arrow thickness, or architecture-diagram layout. White backgrounds, restrained colors, thin rules, readable grayscale, clear arrow semantics and no collisions are this revision's quality criteria.
