\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:limits}
The current two-scene application run tests the protocol/data path, but both
model candidates fail the configured gates. In the registered-harness run,
six task executions complete a
synthetically approved, readback-verified recipe trial and task closure;
their single run-tagged samples, collected after a 20-s wait initiated after
task completion and evidence queries through the SQLite fallback, all remain
outside the target bands. This supports the tested
application path, not successful process control. The tasks run sequentially
on one simulator instance per scene, and do not establish independent
replication, convergence, or quality improvement. The historical four-process
study used a scripted direct-node interface; profile checks used mock I/O. Controlled
profile/binding adaptation, cross-harness portability, normal online DAQ
backend behavior, and physical transfer remain untested.

The single-trial recipe case saved but did not apply a recipe; its cadence-bypassed stop does not prove
simultaneous freshness, and a separate default-paced check missed target and
failed restoration. Approval records do not measure operator effectiveness
\cite{parasuramanmanzey2010complacency,parasuraman2000model,bainbridge1983ironies};
restricted tool access does not establish deployment security
\cite{greshake2023injection,liu2024pinjection,iec62443-3-3} or replace
independently engineered PLC/SIS protection \cite{iec61511}.

Further evaluation needs a versioned build, matched scenes, a predeclared
signal-pair/excitation plan, default-paced recipe baselines, and physical
plant trials.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
AgentWorkShop connects collaborative harnesses, production context, model
services, semantic I/O, and node/recipe workflows while retaining distinct
authorization, command-confirmation, and observation records. Current
simulator evidence verifies selected integration paths: the two-scene
application gate rejects both candidates, while all six registered-agent task
executions
complete with verified command receipts and run-tagged samples. None of the
six post-trial samples reaches its target band. The results support the
framework's tested software integration, but do not establish optimization
success, process convergence, cross-harness portability, normal online DAQ
performance, recipe recovery, physical-plant transfer, or comparative control
benefit.
