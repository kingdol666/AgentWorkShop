# AI disclosure and anonymous-review placement

The current review PDF includes an identity-neutral **AI Assistance
Disclosure** section and does not include author-identifying acknowledgments.
This placement documents AI-generated content while preserving the manuscript's
anonymous state. It is a practical interpretation of two instructions that do
not specify how they interact: IEEE's general policy places AI-content
disclosure in Acknowledgments, while the TII checklist says to omit
acknowledgments from the reviewer-facing manuscript. The TII submission portal
instructions should be checked before external submission; if they provide an
editor-only disclosure field, use it as well. In the accepted, identified
version, move the statement to Acknowledgment if that is required by the
submission or production instructions.

The current manuscript statement is:

> OpenAI ChatGPT assisted with manuscript organization and prose development
> in Sections I–VI and drafted Python source for Figs. 1, 2, and 4. OpenAI
> image-generation tools produced early layout concepts for Figs. 1 and 2;
> the final figures were redrawn as editable vector artwork. No experimental
> records or reported measurements were generated by AI. The authors reviewed
> the final text, code, figures, and evidence and remain responsible for the
> submitted content.
