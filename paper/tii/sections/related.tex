\section{Related Work}
\subsection{Industrial Agents and Closed-Loop Applications}
Agent-based manufacturing control provides a foundation for connecting team
roles to industrial capabilities \cite{leitao2009agentbased}. Industrial
LLM applications span knowledge-graph construction \cite{zhang2026comaikg},
diagnosis \cite{liu2024kgdiag}, PLC generation and verification
\cite{liu2026agents4plc,fakih2024llm4plc}, and digital-twin configuration
\cite{xia2024llmsim}. Natural-language machine control over OPC UA
\cite{hofmann2025nlc}, simulated fault-tolerant control, and twin-supported
fault handling \cite{vyas2026ftc,gill2025llmdt} already combine corrective
actions with process knowledge, deterministic checks, or simulation.
InstructMPC introduces language-derived context into model-predictive control
\cite{wu2025instructmpc}. Agent-based supervision of service-oriented
industrial cyber-physical systems \cite{biskupovic2025supervision} and
LLM-based shopfloor coordination \cite{zhao2026shopfloor} are particularly
close to our setting. Our focus is the connection between deployed-member
authority, source-dependent execution checks, and production-context
attribution, rather than execution checks or agent coordination alone.

\subsection{Orchestration, Industrial Models, and Execution Governance}
AutoGen, LangGraph, and CrewAI organize agent tasks and tools
\cite{wu2023autogen,langgraph2024,crewai2024}; Model Context Protocol
(MCP) and Agent-to-Agent (A2A) standardize tool and inter-agent interfaces \cite{mcp2024,a2a2025}. Industrial supervision adds
node semantics, driver behavior, and operating context. Our
organization of lines, products, recipes, and runs draws on ISA-95 work units,
product definitions, and performance records, and the ISA-88 master-recipe
concept \cite{isa95,isa88}, without implementing their full procedural
models. Semantic asset models and gateway digital twins abstract
heterogeneous equipment \cite{iso23247,iec63278,gautam2025iiot}; we address
write governance over that abstraction.

Permissions, range validation, approval, and audit trails have counterparts
in DCS/SCADA/MES practice and IEC~62443-3-3 controls \cite{iec62443-3-3}.
We connect these functions to deployed member instances and associate
interventions with judgments across engine boundaries. Formal action shielding
and control-barrier methods \cite{alshiekh2018shielding,ames2017cbf}, and
simplex-style run-time assurance \cite{sha2001simplex}, instead target
protected execution with specified guarantees. Our admission checks and
rule-based backstop do not provide those guarantees. Human supervision also
requires a division of responsibility beyond an approval dialog
\cite{parasuraman2000model,bainbridge1983ironies}.

\subsection{Evaluation of Tools and Industrial Control}
AgentBench and consequential tool-use benchmarks assess agent behavior and
policy compliance \cite{liu2024agentbench,yao2024taubench}; the Tennessee
Eastman Process (TEP) and Secure Water Treatment (SWaT) support
process-monitoring research \cite{downs1993tep,goh2017swat}. Our
evaluation separates source checks, mock-driver probes, software-protocol
integration, and scripted optimization, so each result identifies the
execution layer it tests. Indirect prompt injection
\cite{greshake2023injection} and benchmarked defenses
\cite{liu2024pinjection} motivate testing compromised agents' use of
legitimate tools; adversarial LLM probes remain outside this evaluation.
