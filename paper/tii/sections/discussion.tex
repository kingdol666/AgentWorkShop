\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}
\subsection{Established Properties and Their Boundaries}
The reusable unit here is the deployed member--node capability--production-run
association. It lets one task work in engineering quantities across drivers while
execution and compensation stay attributable, and it lets the biaxial mission move
three line sections under one objective without plant-specific routing code. It
does not measure the effort to author a scenario or integrate a
protocol the platform does not already speak.

Proposal, admission, and disposition stay distinguishable: a recorded
\texttt{keep} is the agent's judgment, a matching
readback is a driver-level observation, and sustained process convergence is a
separate experimental outcome. The governed-write path concentrates range and
recipe-window checks, ownership, cooldown, and readback. Observation-quality
gating is a framework-level extension this paper does not deliver; until it
exists, the historical LLM scores cannot be compared with the deterministic
ones: their writes executed as designed, but their judgments were formed over
scoring inputs and temporal windows that diverged
from the plant's actual state (Sec.~\ref{sec:repro}).

\subsection{Implementation and Evidence Boundaries}
Cross-agent in-window oscillations, shared-node
contention over longer horizons, and availability during storage failure remain
unevaluated. Approval reduces permitted automation without establishing endpoint security,
protecting against a privileged compromised harness, or validating approved
engineering intent; least-privilege deployment and network separation remain
separate responsibilities \cite{iec62443,nist80082}. Governed writes are
defense-in-depth at the basic process control system layer, not a certified safety
instrumented function: their safety argument presupposes independent regulatory
limits and a protection layer in the IEC 61511 sense \cite{iec61511}, and the
platform implements no detection or degraded-mode response when such layers are
absent, which is an integrator responsibility. Manual-binding approval workload
grows with the frequency of agent proposals, and approval fatigue and
over-reliance are documented risks
\cite{parasuraman2000model,bainbridge1983ironies,leesee2004trust,parasuramanmanzey2010complacency}; policy granularity over
roles, batch phases, and magnitudes is an extension point, not a present
capability. None of these mechanisms is enforced outside the platform process, so
they constrain only traffic that passes through it; a deployment therefore needs
independent setpoint limits and interlocks in the controller itself.

Deployment in a real plant carries prerequisites this paper does not discharge.
Placement across the IT/OT hierarchy and the assignment of IEC 62443 zones and
conduits are integrator decisions \cite{iec62443}. A deployment design must also
answer the platform's failure modes, including the approval channel, alarm paths,
and open records left by a core crash, and must pin engine model versions where
regulated industries require reproducible behavior. The single-host Windows
capacity boundary is likewise untested.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
\system{} integrates multi-agent coordination with heterogeneous industrial
supervision through explicit node capabilities and shared production context. The
architecture keeps coordination and supervision cooperating without becoming one atomic control
function. Reference archive $B$ provides three
complementary results: four writable stacks reject all predefined
out-of-constraint writes with no legal-write blocks; a fixed controller reaches
$J/J^*=0.963$--$0.972$ across three seeds; and a scripted team mission uses three
governed writes across two protocols to bring biaxial-film thickness from 28.10 to
25.68\,$\mu$m. Seven of the ten reportable same-command reruns (of eleven
archived, including the reference) attain the band, the reported run
among them; the failed reruns end near zero thickness with no check failing and the mission
checks downgraded to warnings, and the suite's hard gate does not flag them. These results demonstrate the tested integration and governance
paths only. Physical validation, stronger execution consistency, controlled agent comparisons,
and observation-quality gating remain open.
