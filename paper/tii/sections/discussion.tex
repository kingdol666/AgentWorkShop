\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\subsection{Scope and Limits}
\label{sec:limits}
The pipeline constrains the \emph{action language} of agents, not the quality of their choices: proposals still derive from LLM competence, context quality, and tool ergonomics, and the unbounded statistical components (LLM decisions, with deterministic rule fallbacks) bottom out at the rules' behavior. Bounded mechanisms---interlock, readback, chain limit---absorb bad proposals. The backstop's $K{=}2$ caps a node's lifetime automatic rollbacks, an engineering limit exercised only up to one in the drill. The invariants borrow IEC~61511's separation of basic control from safety functions \cite{iec61511} but remain engineering properties, not certified safety functions: the by-construction guarantee holds within the sanctioned path of Sec.~\ref{sec:hitl}; \system{} does not replace certified instrumentation. The completed evidence covers the artifact audit, live-instance invariant verification, a dynamically exercised rollback, real-protocol scenarios on both scenarios, the pipeline-tier ablation, and one plant domain at L1/L4; the remaining agent-in-the-loop baselines (general frameworks, human operators, cross-engine replication) are defined in \bench{}'s released protocol rather than reported here---the measured evidence covers \emph{governed supervisory control}, not open-ended plant engineering. Three lessons may transfer: single-entry writes are the keystone; judgment/execution separation pays twice; deterministic rehearsal against an offline-optimum oracle surfaces defects before hardware.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We presented \system{}, an integration framework embedding LLM multi-agent teams natively into the supervisory fabric of protocol-heterogeneous production lines---one process, one data domain, physical semantics instead of register addresses, team-level node binding, configuration-only commissioning---with \bench{}, a reproducible evaluation protocol where every agent-issued write passes through a single governed pipeline defining three safety invariants over the accepted-action language, independent of engine. A governed closed-loop benchmark on a seeded cast-film line recovers 97.0--97.2\,\% (quoted run) of the offline grid optimum in two iterations and at most two governed writes per seed (per-seed $J/J^{*}\in[0.962,0.973]$ across released runs); the backstop's rollback is exercised end-to-end on a live breach; and four LLM-agent campaigns actuate the same loop over real transports with every write attributed, reaching a median 0.76 of the optimum (propose/dispose in action).
