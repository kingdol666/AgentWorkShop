\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\subsection{Scope and Limits}
\label{sec:limits}
The pipeline constrains the \emph{action language} of agents; it does not make agents good at choosing actions. Proposal quality still derives from LLM competence, context quality, and tool ergonomics: bounded mechanisms (interlock, readback, chain limit) absorb bad proposals, while the unbounded statistical components (LLM decisions, with deterministic rule fallbacks) sit in a tier whose worst case is the rules' performance. The invariants borrow IEC~61511's separation of basic control from safety functions \cite{iec61511} but remain engineering properties, not certified safety functions: the by-construction guarantee holds within the sanctioned path of Sec.~IV-D, and \system{} does not replace certified instrumentation. The completed evidence covers the artifact audit, live-instance invariant verification, the backstop's dynamically exercised rollback (chain limit $K{=}2$ still specified), real-protocol scenarios on two plant scenarios, the pipeline-tier ablation, and one plant domain at L1/L4; the agent-in-the-loop campaigns (E1b--E9) remain specified---\bench{} measures \emph{governed supervisory control}, not open-ended plant engineering. Three lessons may transfer: single-entry writes are the keystone; judgment/execution separation pays twice; deterministic rehearsal with an offline-optimum oracle surfaced defects before hardware attachment.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We presented \system{}, an integration framework embedding LLM multi-agent teams natively into the supervisory fabric of protocol-heterogeneous production lines---one process, one data domain, physical semantics instead of register addresses, team-level node binding, configuration-only commissioning---with \bench{}, a reproducible evaluation protocol. Every agent-issued write is forced through a single governed pipeline defining three safety invariants over the accepted-action language, independent of engine. A governed closed-loop benchmark on a seeded cast-film line recovers 97.0--97.2\,\% of the offline grid optimum in two iterations and at most two governed writes per seed ($J/J^{*}\in[0.966,0.973]$ over repetitions); the backstop's rollback is exercised end-to-end on a live breach; and four LLM-agent campaigns actuate the same loop over real transports with every write attributed, reaching a median 76\,\% of the optimum---a measured shortfall that motivates the propose/dispose split rather than being papered over.
