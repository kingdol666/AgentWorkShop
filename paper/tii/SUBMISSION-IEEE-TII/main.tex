\title{Grading Machine-Originated Plant Writes: Separately Inspectable Evidence for Industrial Agent Teams}

\author{Anonymous Authors}
\markboth{IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics, Submitted Manuscript}{Grading Machine-Originated Plant Writes}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Large language model (LLM) agents increasingly propose control changes on
industrial plants, yet the safeguards surrounding each
proposal---write authority, active limits, human approval, device
acceptance---are rebuilt per deployment inside application code, so a task
reported complete can coexist with a missed process objective.
AgentWorkShop, a governed supervisory execution layer, carries every
agent-originated write along one path:
admission against declared, versioned limits, approval where required,
actuation over semantic write-control nodes, graded
acknowledgment by independent post-write read, verdict recording, and
compensation---each stage a separately inspectable evidence state
attributed to its originating actor. Team identities reach
data-acquisition and write-control nodes only through explicit
member--node capabilities, and prompt content is never admissible as
authority; a fail-safe-manual line master switch
intercepts approval-free recipe dispatch, and process models, objectives,
and policies stay external to the governed
path. Each write is graded readback-verified, transport-acknowledged, or
unverified by what an independent read observes, never by the transport
receipt alone. Four process scenarios---injection molding, wastewater
treatment, annealing, and biaxially oriented polyethylene terephthalate
(BOPET) film---and a cast-film reference line exercise the layer across
protocol-interception drills, agent-team missions, and a deployment-scale
regression campaign on one multi-line instance: intercepting and
attributing, grading honestly, pacing automation, mediating approval, and
recovering on record. The evaluation is simulation-tier (simulator-backed,
not physical plants); no optimization-benefit or safety-assurance claim is
made.
\end{abstract}
