\title{A Governed Supervisory Execution Layer for LLM-Agent Plant Writes}

\author{Anonymous Authors}
\markboth{IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics, Submitted Manuscript}{Governed Execution for LLM-Agent Plant Writes}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
\looseness=-1 Large language model (LLM) agents increasingly propose control
changes on industrial plants, yet the safeguards around each
proposal---write authority, active limits, human approval, device
acceptance---are rebuilt per deployment, so a completed task can coexist with
a missed process objective. This article presents a governed supervisory
execution layer that carries agent-originated writes along one path:
admission against declared, versioned limits; approval where required;
actuation over semantic write-control nodes; acknowledgment graded by
post-write observation, not the transport receipt; and verdict recording with
compensation, each stage a separately inspectable, attributed evidence state.
Team identities reach nodes only through explicit member--node capabilities,
prompt content is never authority, and a fail-safe-manual
recipe-dispatch master switch intercepts approval-free dispatch. The layer is
organized as cooperating planes---team coordination, protocol integration,
governance, production context, and extension---and ships as one service with
a typed software development kit and an industrial tool surface.
Evaluation spans injection molding, wastewater treatment, annealing, and
biaxially oriented polyethylene terephthalate film, plus a cast-film reference
line, across interception drills, agent-team missions, a deterministic
controller loop, concurrent missions, and a deployment-scale campaign.
Out-of-window proposals
are rejected at the attributable admission layer, the governed path closes
with graded evidence, and a success-flag rule reports success where grading
reports unverified. The evaluation is simulation-tier: it establishes
mechanism behavior and evidence integrity, not optimization benefit or safety
assurance.
\end{abstract}
