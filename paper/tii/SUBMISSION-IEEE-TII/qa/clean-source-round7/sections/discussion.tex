\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:limits}
R20 demonstrates the bounded registered-harness path across the two tested
simulator scenes: tasks completed with synthetic approval and verified
readback, and all six records passed the task-to-approval-to-receipt-to-sample
link checks. R21 exercised one deterministic fixture task per scene and
passed its 31 runner assertions, including the run-tagged sample checks. No
R20 post-trial sample reached its target band. The 20-s sample delay began
after receipt/readback verification; the runner checked task state after the
sample query, and no task-completion-to-sample interval is retained. R13
separately contributes exploratory protocol and data-lineage evidence.
Together, these runs support only the configured software paths; they do not
establish process-control efficacy or portability.

R20 uses one simulator instance per scene and does not establish independent
replication, convergence, or quality improvement. Its single post-trial
sample per execution is not a response trajectory or a
task-completion-to-sample latency measurement. Controlled profile/binding
adaptation, cross-harness portability, normal online DAQ backend behavior, and
physical transfer remain untested. The synthetic approval records do not
measure operator effectiveness
\cite{parasuramanmanzey2010complacency,parasuraman2000model,bainbridge1983ironies};
restricted tool access does not establish deployment security
\cite{greshake2023injection,liu2024pinjection,iec62443-3-3} or replace
independently engineered PLC/SIS protection \cite{iec61511}.

Future evaluation should use independent simulator replications, a controlled
configuration-change test, the normal online DAQ backend, and physical-plant
trials. These tests are needed to assess benefits beyond the configured paths
reported here.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
AgentWorkShop provides a modular reference implementation that connects
collaborative tasks, production context, operation checks, semantic I/O, and
route-specific records. R20 demonstrates a live-agent task-to-sample trace in
two simulator scenes, with complete linkage checks for six executions; none
of its post-trial samples reached target. R21 separately verifies the
deterministic task route in both scenes, while R13 supplies exploratory
protocol and data-path evidence. These results establish behavior of the
tested software paths, not process-control efficacy, comparative advantage,
or portability.
