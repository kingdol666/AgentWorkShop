D. Approval-Context Guard and Ablation
   The production handler blocked twelve one-field changes to
the approved tuple (three each to line, product, recipe, and run)
before a mock write or execution record. A queued context
mutation between grant revalidation and the caller’s final
synchronous check was also blocked before recipe_apply
dispatch. In the test-only ablation, removing the tuple compar-
ison while retaining the other checks allowed six product/run
changes through; each produced two mock writes and one
execution record. The remaining six line/recipe changes stayed
blocked. Across seven suites, 566/566 local unit tests passed,
including the targeted approval tests.
   Within the tested software state, the ablation isolates the
tuple comparison: it rejects the product/run changes that the
retained checks alone admitted. It does not demonstrate atom-
icity across network or device I/O, safe physical operation,
operator performance, or end-to-end DAQ integrity, and the
test count is not a failure-rate estimate.
