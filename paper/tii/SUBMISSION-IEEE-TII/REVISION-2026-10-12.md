## Framing fix — three seats converged on this as CRITICAL

`mechanisms.tex`, backstop paragraph. A caller's own `keep`/`rollback` closes the record
and stands the backstop down, so the platform's **only automatic recovery action is
contingent on the verdict of the identity it exists to contain** — reachable with no
credential theft and no policy violation, entirely inside the paper's own threat model.
The mechanism was disclosed; the adversarial consequence was not. Now stated, together
with:

- "a record-integrity check … not an independent protection layer" (was "the platform's
  safety net");
- that availability is contingent on a verdict being recorded **and on observations
  continuing to arrive**;
- one-step restore semantics (`A→B→C` reverts to `B`; the admitted baseline is not
  restored).

`mechanisms.tex` also narrows the learned-feasibility claim to **decidability and
replayability** and cites the safe-optimization class (`garcia2015safe`, `sui2015safe`) —
a load-bearing comparative claim that was previously uncited.
