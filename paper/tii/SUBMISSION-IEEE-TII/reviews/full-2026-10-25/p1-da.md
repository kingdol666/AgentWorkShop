## Contract Paraphrase

D1 — Challenge whether the study’s design, handling of data, statistical reporting, and reproducibility details are strong enough to support its claims.

D2 — Challenge factual accuracy: check whether claims match domain evidence, prior work is represented correctly, and technical terms and results are used without error.

D3 — Stress-test whether the central thesis is consistent, the evidence supports its claims, and no fallacy undermines the main argument.

D4 — Challenge whether readers from adjacent fields can follow the framing and definitions, and whether interdisciplinary implications are adequately supported.

D5 — Challenge whether the manuscript’s organization, explanations, figures, tables, and venue conventions make the work clear and usable to readers.

D6 — Challenge whether the manuscript fits the configured venue and offers an original, significant contribution suited to its readership.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Look for contradictions in the thesis, unsupported steps between evidence and claims, and reasoning errors that weaken the central argument.
what_triggers_block: The central argument has contradictions or inferential gaps that require substantial restructuring before its claims can be supported.
what_triggers_warn: Some evidence-to-claim links or scope qualifications are weak, but the central thesis remains supportable.
what_triggers_fatal: The central thesis depends on irreconcilable premises or lacks any viable evidentiary basis, leaving its core argument untenable.
