## Contract Paraphrase

D1 — methodology_rigor: This dimension concerns whether the study’s design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility support meet the field’s review standards; it is outside the domain reviewer’s scoring remit.

D2 — domain_accuracy: This dimension concerns whether the paper’s claims match current domain evidence, represent prior work correctly, and use domain terminology and results accurately. I will assess it for factual or representational errors and distinguish issues that undermine core claims from those that are material but repairable.

D3 — argumentative_coherence: This dimension concerns whether the thesis is internally consistent and supported by evidence, without reasoning errors that undermine its central argument; it is outside the domain reviewer’s scoring remit.

D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance: This dimension concerns whether framing, definitions, and implications are accessible to readers from adjacent fields and whether interdisciplinary claims have support; it is outside the domain reviewer’s scoring remit.

D5 — writing_and_structure: This dimension concerns manuscript organization, clarity, figure and table quality, and conformity with venue conventions; it is outside the domain reviewer’s scoring remit.

D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution: This dimension concerns fit with the configured venue and whether the manuscript offers an original, significant contribution suited to its readership; it is outside the domain reviewer’s scoring remit.

## Scoring Plan

### D2: domain_accuracy
dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: Check whether domain-specific claims, descriptions of prior work, terminology, and stated results align with the relevant evidence and established meanings.
what_triggers_block: A material domain error or misrepresentation undermines the reliability of a central claim, but the issue appears correctable without invalidating the paper’s core contribution.
what_triggers_warn: A meaningful but localized domain inaccuracy, imprecise term, or incomplete representation of prior work needs correction while leaving the central claims reliable.
what_triggers_fatal: A fundamental domain error or pervasive misrepresentation makes the central claims untenable or invalidates the paper’s core contribution.
