## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor): Editorial oversight should leave the assessment of study design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility to the methodology reviewer, who determines whether these meet the field’s review standards.

D2 (domain_accuracy): Editorial oversight should leave verification of domain claims, prior-work descriptions, terminology, and reported facts to the domain reviewer.

D3 (argumentative_coherence): Editorial oversight should leave the evaluation of thesis consistency, evidentiary support, and logic that could undermine the central argument to the assigned reviewers.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance): Editorial oversight should leave assessment of accessibility to adjacent-field readers and support for interdisciplinary implications to the perspective reviewer.

D5 (writing_and_structure): Editorial oversight should assess whether the manuscript is clearly organized and explained, its figures and tables are usable, and its presentation follows venue conventions.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution): Editorial oversight should assess whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and offers an original, significant contribution suited to that venue’s readership.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Assess organization, clarity of exposition, figure and table quality, and adherence to venue conventions.
what_triggers_block: Structural or exposition problems repeatedly prevent readers from following the argument or connecting its evidence to its claims.
what_triggers_warn: Local organization, clarity, figure, table, or convention issues are present but do not prevent evaluation of the argument.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Assess fit with the configured venue and whether originality and significance are appropriate for its readership.
what_triggers_block: The contribution is insufficiently differentiated or its significance is inadequately supported, requiring substantial revision to establish its venue-relevant value.
what_triggers_warn: The contribution is broadly relevant to the venue, but its distinctiveness or significance needs a limited clarification.
what_triggers_fatal: The work is outside the configured venue’s scope and has no credible path to a venue-relevant original and significant contribution.
