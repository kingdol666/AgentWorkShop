## Contract Paraphrase

D1 — methodology_rigor: The study’s design, treatment of data, statistical account, and support for reproducibility should meet the standards expected in its field.

D2 — domain_accuracy: The paper’s claims should agree with relevant domain evidence, represent prior work correctly, and use domain terminology and results accurately.

D3 — argumentative_coherence: The main argument should hold together internally, have adequate support, and avoid reasoning errors that undermine its central case.

D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance: Readers from adjacent fields should be able to follow the framing, definitions, and implications, and interdisciplinary claims should have adequate support.

D5 — writing_and_structure: The manuscript should be clearly organized and explained, with figures and tables that communicate effectively and presentation that follows the venue’s conventions.

D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution: The manuscript should suit the configured venue and offer an original, meaningful contribution for its intended readership.

## Scoring Plan

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance
dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Whether readers from adjacent fields can follow the framing, definitions, and implications, and whether interdisciplinary claims are substantiated.
what_triggers_block: The central framing or definitions are inaccessible to adjacent-field readers, or unsupported interdisciplinary claims prevent them from assessing the paper’s contribution.
what_triggers_warn: A localized accessibility gap or unsupported interdisciplinary claim makes the paper harder to follow but does not prevent adjacent-field readers from assessing its contribution.
