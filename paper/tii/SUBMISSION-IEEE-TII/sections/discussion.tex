\section{Engineering Interpretation}
\label{sec:limits}
The evaluated contribution is configuration-based reuse across industrial
agent settings. Q4 exercises four process configurations, each with one
seed-42 simulator trajectory; all met their declared terminal checks, two
near a boundary. Q1 covers acquisition across five protocol families and
writes on four (RTU-over-TCP was acquisition-only). Q2 contains two live
engine tasks; Q3 uses three seeds and a restricted setpoint grid; Q5 records
782 deterministic assertions across nine software-suite executions; Q6 is
one accelerated recipe trajectory. Together, these archives exercise
strategy, I/O, scene configuration, and workflow integration, but do not
compare optimization performance.

Authorization, admission, approval, and graded confirmation are
cross-cutting operational services within the framework. Their records
connect an actor and production scope to a command receipt and process
observation. They do not measure operator comprehension, complacency, or
responsibility allocation \cite{parasuramanmanzey2010complacency,parasuraman2000model,bainbridge1983ironies}.
The execution boundary is independent of prompts and retrieved documents
\cite{greshake2023injection,liu2024pinjection}; deployment still requires
credential management, endpoint security, and IT/OT separation
\cite{iec62443-3-3}. It complements rather than replaces independently
engineered PLC interlocks and safety-instrumented systems; IEC~61511 governs
the latter's process-safety lifecycle \cite{iec61511}.

\noindent\textit{Experimental scope.}
Process evidence is simulator-based and approvals were scripted. Q1--Q4 use
a historical direct-node interface; Q5 comprises software assertions, not
process samples; Q6 is one October 18 recipe trajectory with cadence
overrides. The archives do not form a joint current-version end-to-end
study, and a complete current exploration-to-process-response test is not
archived. On October 23, the current public interface refused all eight
historical direct-node attempts. A separate current recipe check verified
only the 45-to-50-bar trial, refused later requests at 66 and 132 s under the
default cadence, reached no target, saved no recipe, and failed its
restoration assertion. These checks expose interface boundaries; they do
not repeat the accelerated case. The evidence does not estimate physical
transfer, general plant coverage, hard real-time behavior, fault frequency,
human performance, or comparative benefit. Repeated matched runs, a
current-route end-to-end study, and physical evaluation are needed; process
benchmarks such as Tennessee Eastman \cite{downs1993tep} offer one further
setting.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
AgentWorkShop combines collaborative tasks and replaceable strategy
services with production context, semantic industrial I/O, and distinct
node and recipe workflows. Across the tested simulator scenes and protocol
routes, the implementation exercised configuration-based reuse and
route-specific evidence. These results clarify what the framework connects
and where its current evidence stops; they do not establish general
industrial coverage, physical transfer, or comparative control benefit.
