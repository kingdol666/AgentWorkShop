\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}
\subsection{Industrial-Informatics Interpretation}
One supervisory path carried four different jobs and a deployment-scale
campaign with command closure, verdict, task state, and attainment kept as
separate records; against the nearest systems of Section~\ref{sec:related},
the differentiator is not the LLM---a replaceable strategy slot---but the
production-scoped authority and evidence around its writes, which none of
the six systems combines with reversal through one path.
Deployment-wise, the layer installs as one service beside the plant's
protocol endpoints: a line is onboarded by declaring a blueprint, and
approval clients---web panel or API---receive the HITL cards; PLC protection
and DCS interlocks stay untouched.

\subsection{Validity and Reproducibility}
\looseness=-1 Run identifier, seed, commit, harness fingerprint, and raw artifacts are
fixed, so counts are re-derivable rather than trusted; Q5 adds per-run
ledgers and timelines. Five
qualifications bound what they support: simulation-tier evidence (deterministic per seed, not
validated digital twins; no physical-device campaign, operator study, or
external comparison reported); internal-oracle optima and predicates,
supporting attribution rather than statistical inference; single-host
latencies and near-contemporary runs, bounding repeatability rather than
drift; implemented-but-unexercised capabilities and a single live
provider/model; and harness-adjudicated
approval (human decision latency and quality out of scope).
\subsection{Safety Positioning and Human Oversight}
The threat model is a misbehaving or manipulated agent (wrong or hallucinated
proposals) inside an otherwise trusted platform and plant; the approval dialog,
admission re-checks, and journaling are the containment surfaces, and no
adversarial injection campaign is reported (future work); stolen credentials
and compromised endpoints are the domain of IEC~62443
\cite{iec62443-3-3,nist80082}. Fast regulation, hardware interlocks,
and safety functions remain in the PLC; prompts and retrieved documents are
untrusted text, not authority
\cite{greshake2023injection,liu2024pinjection}---the proposer is confined to
per-binding scope and journaled, attributable actions: a bounded blast
radius, not a protection guarantee.
Admission limits,
acknowledgment grading, and the backstop run with or without human
approval---enforcement never queues behind human attention, while approval
fatigue and automation bias remain human-factors risks
\cite{bainbridge1983ironies}.

\subsection{Deployment Boundaries}
\looseness=-1 Execution and recovery are non-atomic: bookkeeping may fail after actuation, the
pacing windows time writes rather than certify
stability, readback agreement and three-way consistency need not imply process
recovery, and multi-node compensation may partially
succeed; enabled hold heartbeats re-apply the stored setpoint without
re-admission and can trail a narrowed window; oscillation, storage
failure, clock error, and concurrency beyond the four-line campaign remain
unevaluated. In production, bindings govern
sanctioned tools, not arbitrary privileged-engine I/O---least privilege,
endpoint security, and IT/OT separation remain necessary
\cite{iec62443-3-3,nist80082}; \system{} is neither a safety
instrumented function nor a plant-protection substitute \cite{iec61511};
line-scoped approver authorization is still missing, so production needs
segregation of duties and alarm-path failure tests \cite{eemua191}.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
\system{} contributes a governed supervisory execution architecture rather
than a new process optimizer: one stable path from agent-team identities
through semantic nodes, production context, governed writes, graded
acknowledgments, three-way consistency, verdicts, and compensation,
exercised by a \resPhases-phase suite, agent-team missions, a three-seed
loop, four concurrent missions, and consecutive deployment-scale regression
campaigns that complete fully green on a live multi-tenant
instance. Physical-plant deployment,
operator studies, and external-framework comparison remain open; until then,
optimization superiority, autonomous control, and safety assurance stay
unsupported claims.
