\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}
\subsection{Validity and Reproducibility}
\looseness=-1 Seed, manifest, and raw artifacts are fixed; Q5 adds per-run ledgers and
timelines. Five
qualifications bound them: simulation-tier evidence (no physical-device
campaign, operator study, or external comparison); internal-oracle optima and
predicates under an author-implemented, author-adjudicated harness;
single-host latencies and near-contemporary runs; unexercised capabilities and
one live provider/model; and harness-adjudicated approval, with human latency
and quality out of scope.
\subsection{Safety Positioning and Human Oversight}
\looseness=-1 The threat model is a misbehaving or manipulated agent inside
an otherwise trusted platform and plant; approval, admission re-checks, and
journaling are the containment surfaces, and no injection campaign or
operator study is reported. Stolen credentials and compromised endpoints fall
under IEC~62443 \cite{iec62443-3-3}; record-keeping and human-oversight duties
belong to Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 \cite{eu2024aiact}, which the attributed
journal and approval gates serve at the evidence layer, certification out of
scope.
Protection functions remain in the PLC; prompts and retrieved documents are
untrusted text, not authority
\cite{greshake2023injection,liu2024pinjection}. The proposer is confined to
per-binding scope and journaled actions: a bounded blast radius, not a
protection guarantee. Admission, acknowledgment grading, and the backstop run
with or without human approval; enforcement never queues behind human
attention (the deferred-mode exception hands actuation to the approval policy,
Section~\ref{sec:agentloop}). Pacing bounds approval demand; batch release
(Section~\ref{sec:hardening}, leg~5) is the demonstrated fatigue mitigation,
while approval fatigue and automation bias remain human-factors risks
\cite{parasuramanmanzey2010complacency,parasuraman2000model,bainbridge1983ironies}.
A lead agent's delegation-mode choice (Section~\ref{sec:hitl}) is the one
agent-reachable path around per-request approval; capping it at the granter's
binding mode or requiring line-switch confirmation is the queued guard,
alongside the verdict-side guard of Section~\ref{sec:agentloop} (independent
adjudication or a platform-side re-check before \texttt{keep}).

\subsection{Deployment Boundaries}
\looseness=-1 Against the nearest systems of Section~\ref{sec:related},
the differentiator is not the LLM---a replaceable strategy slot---but the
production-scoped authority and evidence around its writes: no nearest
system reports that combination on one path. The contribution is a
framework claim---design, contracts, evidence semantics---assessed by
construction, mechanism ablation, and two falsifiable contrasts (the
window-edge probes and the publish-only receipt), not by outcome superiority;
an outcome-level comparison against SCADA-mediated operation is out of scope.
The layer installs beside the
plant's protocol endpoints, onboards declaratively
(Section~\ref{sec:reuse}), and leaves PLC protection and DCS interlocks
untouched.
\looseness=-1 Execution and recovery are non-atomic: bookkeeping may fail after
actuation, pacing times writes rather than certifying stability, step limits
are advisory, and multi-node compensation may partially succeed. Readback
agreement and three-way consistency do not imply process recovery; hold
heartbeats re-apply the setpoint without re-admission. Oscillation, storage
failure, clock error, concurrency beyond four lines, and split-brain
deployments remain unevaluated; the journal is append-only but not
tamper-evident, so forensic integrity rests on host security and clock skew
propagates into timelines. A supervisor outage halts dispatch; plant staff
retain their DCS/SCADA stations, so the governed path mediates agent-originated
and platform-client writes, not existing operator tooling---an integration
boundary, not a takeover. The next evidence tier is a
hardware-in-the-loop leg against a real PLC or OPC-UA endpoint and community
benchmarks such as Tennessee Eastman \cite{downs1993tep}. \looseness=-1 In production, bindings govern
sanctioned tools, not arbitrary privileged-engine I/O; least privilege,
endpoint security, and IT/OT separation remain necessary
\cite{iec62443-3-3}. The platform is neither a safety
instrumented function nor a plant-protection substitute \cite{iec61511}, and
line-scoped approver authorization is still missing, so production needs
segregation of duties and alarm-path failure tests.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
\looseness=-1 The platform contributes a governed supervisory execution layer, not a
new optimizer: one path from agent-team identities to governed writes, graded
acknowledgment, verdict, and compensation, with admission, approval, and
recovery left as separately inspectable, actor-attributed evidence on every
agent-originated write---the actionable unit, independently inspectable and
revocable. Physical-plant deployment, operator studies, and
external comparison remain open; optimization benefit, autonomous control,
and safety assurance stay unsupported claims.

\vspace{2pt}\noindent\textit{Data availability and disclosure.}
\looseness=-1 Artifacts are archived per run; the platform and simulators are
publicly available (links withheld for double-blind review;
Section~\ref{sec:arch}). The live engine-turn transcripts survive only as
recorded outcomes; the write-evidence suite is rerunnable. AI assistants
supported editing under author direction; the authors remain responsible for
all claims.
