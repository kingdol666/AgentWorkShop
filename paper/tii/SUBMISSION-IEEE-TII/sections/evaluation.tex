\section{Experimental Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}
The evaluation asks whether one governed supervisory path can be exercised,
inspected, and repeated across heterogeneous industrial jobs, not whether one
optimizer beats another. Five questions organize the evidence: protocol
closure and admission-gate attribution (Q1), the agent-team workflow (Q2), a
deterministic controller loop (Q3), four concurrent missions (Q4), and
deployment-scale regression with write-evidence verification (Q5). Each
question is reported as procedure and archived result. Q2--Q4
occupy the strategy slot (scripted policies, with two live engine turns in
Q2); Q5 stresses the hardened platform under load.

\subsection{Testbed, Evidence Snapshot, and Scope}
\label{sec:testbed}
\looseness=-1 The evaluation draws on two tiers of one codebase. The
\emph{archived snapshot} for Q1--Q4 is an integrated run (seed \resSeed)
with raw reports and an ablation companion run. Its
standing preset is a cast-film reference line tuned through the
die-head heater setpoint (objective $J$: thickness conformance,
defects, energy, throughput). Each mission contributes one seed-42
trajectory: Q1, Q2, and Q4 are single-trace case studies; the deterministic
loop adds seeds 43--44, and repeat evidence comes from Q5's deployment-tier
campaigns---a different tier, not a re-run.

\looseness=-1 The \emph{deployment-scale tier} for Q5 is a live instance
carrying \resLinesLive{} provisioned lines, \resChannelsLive{} agent
channels, \resDaqOnline{} online DAQ nodes, \resWriteNodesLive{} write
nodes, \resRecipesLive{} recipes, and \resBindingsLive{}
member--node bindings---simulator-backed, not physical
plants; \emph{multi-line} means many provisioned lines and channels on one
host.
\looseness=-1 The tier's benchmark line---distinct from the cast-film
reference---is a physics-based injection-molding cell (16 DAQ nodes, 6
control nodes over five protocol families plus MES, four exercised for
governed writes); all Q5
numbers come from archived per-run reports and timelines
(three passes of three runs each),
with \resLinesRunning{} of those lines running and \resChannelsLive{} agent
channels active. One timeline fact matters:
acknowledgment grading and the master switch came with the later
hardening pass, \emph{after} the Q1--Q4 snapshot---Q1--Q4 establish the
governed path without them, and Q5 evidences both surfaces; the ablation
isolates the window interlock, untouched by that pass.
The harness sets that line's recipe-dispatch master switch to
\emph{automatic} by user-side confirmation---the automated path is the
object under test---and verifies manual-mode interception separately
(legs~1 and~5).


\subsection{Test Procedure and Reported Quantities}
\label{sec:procedure}
\looseness=-1 \emph{Evidence unit.} Every execution emits per-assertion check
records, phase records, per-line metrics, a timestamped timeline, and a
rendered report; a claim below is read from those artifacts, and a run passes
only when every check in its declared surface passes.

\looseness=-1 \emph{Q1--Q4 procedure.} One integrated suite runs 20 named
phases in fixed order, each owning one claim: P0--P2 commission the testbed
(simulator bootstrap; preset with an offline optimum $W^*$; up to five
protocol lines); P3--P4 (Q1) exercise acquisition and the write path (DAQ through
real drivers; governed write and readback; window-edge interception; a
tool-level loop without an LLM); P4b--P4f exercise the governance surfaces
(records and rollback, HITL, audit, recipe life cycle, parameter mapping);
P4m, P5, P5b, and P10 (Q2) run missions (scripted team task; two live engine turns
and a recipe save; the multi-node BOPET mission); P6--P8b (Q3) close the loop and
test reuse (three-seed controller benchmark, vector and image frames, a
configuration-only second preset, the backstop drill); P9 and P11 (Q4) check
subsystems and four concurrent missions. The suite is an author-defined
regression instrument, not an external certification.

\looseness=-1 \emph{Q5 procedure.} The deployment tier runs the one-command
pipeline: S0 pre-check; S1 API surface; S2 multimodal ingestion with MES
dual-mode; S3 governance negative paths; S4 closed loop; S5 fine-tuning; S6
diagnostics; S7 report. Three passes of three runs each (nine executions)
replay the same surface, and a seven-leg suite
(Section~\ref{sec:hardening}) covers write-evidence and regression surfaces.

\looseness=-1 \emph{Controls and quantities.} The admission control relaxes
the mechanisms: the six window-edge probes that full governance rejects
(HTTP~400) return 200 \emph{executed} under the relaxed arm
(Table~\ref{tab:ablation})---a success-flag design reports those writes as
accepted. The evidence control holds the receipt and varies the reporting
rule (Section~\ref{sec:ack}): applied to the same publish-only dispatch, a
success-flag rule reports success while the graded rule reports unverified
(Section~\ref{sec:hardening}, leg~4). Reported quantities are interception
(rejected probes over probes issued, layer named), acknowledgment (terminal
grade per parameter; batch summary), consistency (maximum deviation among
recipe, readback, and mirror), attainment (signed margin, decided by an
archived check), latency (server-side span with sample size), and
repeatability (checks passed over checks run); with $n\approx6$ writes per
line, distributions are medians with observed maxima, not percentiles.
Fig.~\ref{fig:loop} summarizes the closed-loop instrument.

\subsection{Q1: Protocol Closure and Admission-Gate Attribution}
\label{sec:protocol}
\looseness=-1 \emph{Protocol closure.} On the five protocol lines the suite
stores \resDaqSamples{} DAQ samples through real drivers and dispatches
governed writes over four protocol families---about \resWriteNperLine{}
writes per line, with median command latency \resPfiftyRange{} and observed
maximum \resPninetyfiveRange{}. The RTU-over-TCP satellite is
acquisition-only---readback confirmation where supported---with one
admission-record-verdict path per request. Each writable line rejects 6/6
out-of-window probes (24/24) while accepting its legal write.

\looseness=-1 \emph{Drills.} Two drills separate truth paths one success flag
would collapse: a frozen-measurement drill (write and readback consistent,
measurement out of window) ends with a system-judged backstop rollback
\resBackstop{} later, and a record-rollback drill restores the prior value by
a separately admitted, readback-confirmed write. A second preset with
different signals and dynamics is commissioned by configuration alone; across
both presets 33 probes are rejected on seven writable lines (24+9)---the same
admission code discriminating on unseen signal maps. The parameter layer
makes the interval geometry of Eq.~(\ref{eq:admission}) observable
(Fig.~\ref{fig:hitl}a); a manual binding suspends a request visibly and
unblocks it on approval (latency \resHitlLatency, harness-adjudicated).

\looseness=-1 \emph{Ablation.} The ablation replays the same probes on a
companion run and a standalone fixture (six probe classes per arm, three
deterministic repetitions). Table~\ref{tab:ablation} reports four arms: with
the interlock, 18/18 probes are rejected with or without readback; without
it, only \resNoInterlockIntercept{} reject and the six window-class probes
execute and are journaled---prevention is the interlock's job, and the
readback check only detects. The hard range is structurally
unbypassable---all four governed routes re-admit it at one journaling entry
point, per request (Section~\ref{sec:nodeexec})---and the soft window is its
removable part.

\subsection{Q2: The Agent-Team Optimization Workflow and Its Results}
\label{sec:agentteam}
\looseness=-1 \emph{Missions.} The workflow ran end to end on the cast-film
line and then a larger BOPET line (Section~\ref{sec:reuse}); a scripted policy
issued the writes. On the single-node
mission one write sufficed (\resMissionFrom\,$\rightarrow\,$\resMissionTo\,$^{\circ}$C
against target \resMissionTarget\,$^{\circ}$C,
settled at \resMissionPv\,$^{\circ}$C, journal-attributed, task
\texttt{COMPLETED}).
The BOPET mission is the multi-node case: nine devices and 49 signals
(\resBiaxSp{} writable, \resBiaxDaq{} readable), all ensure-pass created.
Three
governed writes on three distinct nodes---casting speed
$32\!\rightarrow\!33.8$\,m/min, fast-roll speed
$118\!\rightarrow\!120.3$\,m/min, and rail width $3000\!\rightarrow\!3035$\,mm---
move the archived thickness trajectory from \resBiaxStart{} to
\resBiaxFinal\,$\mu$m against
$\resBiaxTarget\pm\resBiaxTol$\,$\mu$m, $0.04$\,$\mu$m inside the upper
bound. The structured attainment check evaluates
$|25.66-25.0|=0.66\leq0.7$ and passes, as the regenerated summary now
reports; the $0.04$\,$\mu$m margin is four $0.01$\,$\mu$m quantization
steps, a boundary pass rather than a robust process win. Each
write opened a record, received \texttt{keep}, and left an
agent-attributed journal entry; the parent task reached \texttt{COMPLETED}.

\looseness=-1 \emph{Live engine turns.} Two engine turns (omp adapter,
glm-5.3-flash) drove the same slot on a scratch line---a bounded write
(\resLlmWall), a goal-driven descent settling at
\resGoalFinal\,$\mu$m against a harness-supplied target ($52.0\pm0.8$\,$\mu$m,
in the task text), and a recipe save
with attributed authorship (\resGoalWall). They show an engine
occupying the slot, not agent quality---the run's pass criterion is a
completion marker in the transcript.

\subsection{Q3: The Deterministic Closed Loop}
\label{sec:controller}
\looseness=-1 \emph{Closed loop.} On the cast-film line, seeds 42--44 each issue two governed writes without rejection,
raising the objective $J$ from $J_{0}\approx\resJzero$ toward its
grid optimum ($J/J^{*}=\resControllerMin$--\resControllerMax{} against
$J^{*}=\resJstar$; the grid enumerates the admissible zone-temperature,
screw-speed, and line-speed setpoints, so $J^{*}$ lower-bounds the true
optimum and the ratio upper-bounds $J/J_{\text{true}}$). The claim is
narrow: the governed path passes a conventional controller's writes through
unmodified---admissible, journaled, read back---so this evidences
transparency of handling, not a performance delta.

\subsection{Q4: Four Process Missions}
\label{sec:scenarios}
\looseness=-1 \emph{Four missions.} The campaign runs four blueprint-provisioned lines concurrently; in this run
(seed 42) the archived structured checks evaluate true for all four, from
subsequent observations rather than task state, and two are boundary passes
(Fig.~\ref{fig:missions}). Injection molding is the minimal case: two writes (hold pressure
45$\rightarrow$58.2\,bar, hold time 6$\rightarrow$10.64\,s) move part weight
from \resInjStart{} to \resInjFinal\,g against a $32.5\pm0.35$\,g window, a
boundary pass $0.02$\,g above the lower bound; BOPET is the multi-node case
of Section~\ref{sec:agentteam}. Both close with task state and predicate
agreeing. Wastewater treatment (anaerobic--anoxic--oxic) is the
write-heaviest mission (\resWwtpWrites{} governed writes across distinct
nodes---the Section~\ref{sec:nodeexec} spacing is per node): dissolved oxygen
rises from \resWwtpStart{} to \resWwtpFinal\,mg/L, inside the
$3.4\pm0.6$\,mg/L attainment window (Fig.~\ref{fig:missions}b). All five
effluent guards remain inside their hard limits; four finish with a wider
margin than at baseline, while pH narrows from 6.59 to 6.38 (limit 6.0).
Attainment is therefore on the controlled DO quantity, not on guard margin. Its
most informative events are blower steps that were admissible and journaled
yet lost compliance margin; the verdict stage
reverted them---a bad proposal is rejected in the evidence, not at the
admission gate. Annealing starts far outside the hardness window
(\resAnnealStart\,HV at 140\,m/min); zone-3 temperature, one
zone-2 trim, and two
line-speed steps hold $95\pm6$\,HV (terminal \resAnnealFinal{} HV,
$0.92$ HV inside the upper bound) while raising line speed by
\resAnnealGain, and a further attempt at \resAnnealSpeedTried\,m/min is
reverted for leaving the margin---on record. Mission clocks (\resInjWall,
\resWwtpWall, \resAnnealWall; BOPET's unarchived) are simulated durations
under accelerated dynamics; platform behavior is identical across the four
while the number of writes varies sevenfold (2, 15, 5, and 3).

\subsection{Q5: Deployment-Scale Regression and Write-Evidence Campaign}
\label{sec:hardening}
The final lens stresses the platform: the hardening pass made
evidence grading, the master switch, and the approval-anchored dispatch
interval of Section~\ref{sec:mech} explicit test surfaces of the
closed-loop, fine-tuning, and write-evidence scenarios.
\looseness=-1 \emph{Full-suite repeatability.} Nine pipeline executions
complete with zero failed checks (Table~\ref{tab:campaign}); the first two
passes wait through the remaining \resCadenceWait{} of the
per-line trial cadence---pacing stays in force under automation.

\looseness=-1 \emph{Closed-loop scenario under governance.} Each run walks
observation, decision (dead-band \resNovDeadband, slope \resNovSlope), a
five-element proposal, HITL approval, batch dispatch, and three-way
consistency (recipe, PLC readback, mirror). The first two passes descend
$66\!\rightarrow\!63$\,bar one governed step per run; the third pass answers a
downward drift in the mirrored weight (\resNovWeights) with a bounded zigzag
(\resNovZigzagFrom$\!\rightarrow\!$\resNovZigzagTo\,bar), deliberate
excitation rather than a correction, and two corrective steps
(\resNovCorrFrom$\!\rightarrow\!$\resNovCorrTo\,bar) answering residual
error ($0.169/0.276$\,g). Every step verifies equal values across the three
sources at maximum consistency error \resConsistencyErr{} ($0.05$\,bar
device, $0.51$\,bar bucketed-mirror tolerances---all legs equal at the
endpoint's recording resolution). The pacing window acts before the human:
in two third-pass runs the proposal is early-rejected by the trial cadence and
re-proposed \resNovCadenceReject{} later; only then is the HITL card raised
and structured approval releases the readback-verified dispatch
(Fig.~\ref{fig:loop}). Approval-to-dispatch takes \resHitlDispatchMs;
observation-to-verification spans \resObsVerifyFast{} with no pending cadence
window and 96.1\,s (third pass) or \resObsVerifyCadence{} (first pass)
with one---the loop's latency is governance, not platform overhead. The
fine-tuning scenario rides the same windows; the diagnostics scenario
exports a 30-min wide table (\resRowsRange{} rows), a data-quality
observation rather than a process result.

\looseness=-1 Latency figures are single-run point estimates: the Q1 approval
latency is the harness's mediation span, not a human response, and Q5's
approval-to-dispatch span is server-side. Only Q3 repeats across seeds, Q5
across consecutive campaigns, and the Q1 ablation deterministically
(Table~\ref{tab:ablation}); the Q1--Q4 missions and the fault-suite grade
outcomes (n=1--2) remain single-trace case studies.

\looseness=-1 \emph{Write-evidence legs.} A dedicated seven-leg suite verifies Sections~\ref{sec:ack}
and~\ref{sec:hitl} directly in legs~(1)--(5): (1)~the recipe-dispatch master switch refuses an unconfirmed manual-to-automatic
transition; (2)~a mock-acknowledged (transport-acknowledged) dispatch is
promoted to readback-verified by post-write re-read;
(3)~a broker cut marks the affected parameter \emph{failed} at the batch
summary while its healthy sibling verifies;
(4)~a publish-only endpoint accepts the write (broker puback,
\texttt{ok=true}) yet stays \emph{unverified} through three verification
attempts; applied to the same receipt, a success-flag rule reports success
while the graded rule reports unverified; (5)~on a manual-mode line an
approval-free binding's dispatch is intercepted into the queue, approval
releasing all six parameters readback-verified; (6)--(7)~API regression and
documentation-consistency suites close the campaign.

\looseness=-1 \emph{Observed grade distribution.} In fault-free operation,
every batch dispatch of the six hardened runs closed \emph{readback-verified}:
structured approval released it and the three-way check read equal values at
\resConsistencyErr{} maximum error (Fig.~\ref{fig:loop}). The non-verified
grades appear only under injected faults, the distribution expected under the
specified grading rule: \resAckFailed{} \emph{failed} (broker unreachable;
healthy sibling verified), \resAckUnverified{} \emph{unverified} (publish-only
endpoint, three attempts), and \resAckPromoted{} parameters promoted from
transport-acknowledged to readback-verified by post-write re-read.
Transport-acknowledged occurs on MES-style endpoints with application-level
acceptance but no readback mapping; MES route tests cover it, but the campaign
archives MES in read mode only---a disclosed coverage gap.

\looseness=-1 Across the five questions, every exercised governed write
produced an attributable evidence state. The window-edge probes and the
publish-only receipt resolved as the grading rule predicts
(Table~\ref{tab:ablation}; Section~\ref{sec:hardening}); the receipt-grading
and master-switch surfaces were exercised in Q5, while Q1--Q4 evidence the
path without them.
