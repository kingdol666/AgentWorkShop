\section{Experimental Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}
The evaluation asks whether one governed supervisory path can be exercised,
inspected, and repeated across heterogeneous industrial jobs, not whether one
optimizer beats another. Five questions organize the evidence: protocol
closure and admission-gate attribution (Q1), the AgentTeam workflow (Q2), a
deterministic controller loop (Q3), four concurrent missions (Q4), and
deployment-scale regression with write-evidence verification (Q5). Q2--Q4
occupy the strategy slot (scripted policies, with two live engine turns in
Q2); Q5 stresses the hardened platform under load. Together the five
exercise every plane of Section~\ref{sec:arch}.

\subsection{Testbed, Evidence Snapshot, and Scope}
\label{sec:testbed}
The evaluation draws on two evidence tiers of one codebase. The
\emph{archived snapshot} for Q1--Q4 is integrated run \resRun{} (seed
\resSeed, commit \resCommit,
harness \resHarness, completed \resEvidenceDate), archived with raw
reports, plus ablation companion \resAblationRun. Its
standing preset is a cast-film extrusion line tuned through the
die-head heater setpoint (objective $J$ weights thickness conformance,
defects, energy, and throughput under melt-temperature and pressure
constraints, larger better). Each mission contributes one seed-42
trajectory: Q1, Q2, and Q4 are single-trace case studies (every claim
each makes is checkable in the archived artifacts), the deterministic loop adds
two in-run traces (seeds 43--44 of the same campaign), and repeat
evidence comes from Q5's consecutive deployment-tier campaigns---a different
tier, not a re-run of these traces.

\looseness=-1 The \emph{deployment-scale tier} for Q5 is a live v0.7.57 instance carrying
\resLinesLive{} provisioned lines (\resLinesRunning{} running), \resChannelsLive{}
active agent channels, \resDaqOnline{} online DAQ nodes,
\resWriteNodesLive{} write nodes, \resRecipesLive{} recipes, and
\resBindingsLive{} member--node bindings---simulator- and bench-backed
objects, not physical plants; \emph{multi-line} means many provisioned
lines and channels on one host.
The benchmark line
is a physics-based injection-molding cell (16 DAQ nodes over the four
modalities, 6 control nodes over five protocol families plus MES); all Q5
numbers come from committed per-run reports, ledgers, and timelines
(October 7--8: hardened-pipeline run \texttt{-20261008045434} plus three
consecutive full-suite runs; October 9: \resNovRuns); mechanisms are
unchanged since the Q1--Q4 snapshot except the October hardening pass's
acknowledgment grading and master switch; the campaign ran under live load.


\subsection{Test Procedure and Campaign Overview}
\label{sec:procedure}
The Q1--Q4 procedure is a \resPhases-phase integrated suite---an author-defined
regression instrument, not an external certification---probing one claim per
phase, from preset loading against the best grid point ($J^{*}=\resJstar$)
through protocol interception and the record, recipe, approval, and
parameter-layer drills (Sections~\ref{sec:protocol} and~\ref{sec:scenarios}).

\looseness=-1 The Q5 procedure is the one-command benchmark pipeline: eight stages
(S0 pre-check; S1 API surface matrix, a positive-and-negative sweep across
authentication, users, channels, tasks, permissions, both node families,
agent tools, HITL, logs, memory, plugins, teams, and notifications; S2
multi-modal ingestion with MES dual-mode; S3 governance negative paths; S4
closed-loop optimization; S5 fine-tuning; S6 diagnostics; S7 report) under a
harness that captures every check and a timestamped event timeline per run,
so each stage exercises one functional surface of
Sections~\ref{sec:arch}--\ref{sec:mech}. The profile has since grown (S1
adds login lockout, S4 the acknowledgment-grade summary); the October 9
campaign re-runs it three times.
No prior system in Table~\ref{tab:nearest} owns a governed write path to
plant endpoints, so the primary comparator is internal, and two controls are
archived. The admission control holds the write path fixed and relaxes its
mechanisms: the same six probes that full governance rejects (HTTP~400)
return 200 \emph{executed} under the relaxed arm
(Section~\ref{sec:protocol}, 18/18$\rightarrow$12/18)---a success-flag
design reports them as accepted writes. The evidence control holds the
receipts and varies the reporting rule (Section~\ref{sec:ack}). The
closed-loop harness also parameterizes the write injection---admin REST
beside the agent's governed \texttt{dcw\_control}---so governance's cost in
convergence and integration effort is measurable; that like-for-like pair is
prepared but not yet executed in the archived campaigns, and no
optimization-superiority claim is made. Fig.~\ref{fig:loop} summarizes the
closed-loop instrument.

\subsection{Q1: Protocol Closure and Admission-Gate Attribution}
\label{sec:protocol}
\looseness=-1 On the five protocol lines the suite stores \resDaqSamples{} DAQ samples
through real drivers and dispatches governed writes over four protocol
families---about \resWriteNperLine{} writes each, p50/p95: \resTcpP/\resTcpTail{}
(Modbus TCP), \resOpcP/\resOpcTail{} (OPC~UA), \resMqttP/\resMqttTail{} (MQTT),
\resHttpP/\resHttpTail{} (HTTP); the RTU-over-TCP
satellite is acquisition-only---recording readback confirmation on the
endpoints that support it, with one admission, record, and verdict path
serving every request. Each writable
line rejects 6/6 out-of-window probes (24/24 intercepted) while accepting
its legal write; the window-interlock drill probes the window edge and
both admission branches.
\looseness=-1 Two drills separate truth paths one success flag would collapse: a
frozen-measurement drill (write and readback consistent, measurement out of
window) ends with a system-judged backstop rollback \resBackstop{} later, and a
record-rollback drill restores the prior value by a separately admitted,
readback-confirmed write. A second preset with different signals and dynamics
is commissioned by configuration alone; across both presets 33 probes are
rejected on seven writable lines, the same admission code discriminating on
unseen signal maps. The parameter
layer makes the interval geometry of Eq.~(\ref{eq:admission}) observable
(Fig.~\ref{fig:hitl}a): a four-layer chain---node $[50,200]$, parameter
$[143.7,156.3]$, product $[146.22,153.78]$, and recipe $[141,159]$\,rpm---narrowing
to one admitted interval, each rejection attributable to its excluding layer;
a manual binding suspends a request visibly and unblocks it on approval
(latency \resHitlLatency, harness-adjudicated and poll-dominated;
Q5 reports the server-side span alone).

\looseness=-1 The ablation replays the same probes on companion run \resAblationRun{}
and a standalone fixture (six probe classes per arm, three deterministic
repetitions with identical counts); it isolates the window interlock,
untouched by the October hardening pass. Full governance and the no-readback arm reject 18/18;
without the window interlock (alone or with the readback check) only
\resNoInterlockIntercept{} reject and the six window-class probes execute
and are journaled---prevention is the interlock's job; a relaxed
configuration degrades from prevention to detection, with the readback check
retained to make written-but-unconfirmed commands visible. The hard range is
structurally unbypassable---all four governed routes re-admit it at one
journaling entry point, per request (Section~\ref{sec:nodeexec})---and the
soft window is its removable part.

\subsection{Q2: The AgentTeam Optimization Workflow and Its Results}
\label{sec:agentteam}
\looseness=-1 The workflow ran end to end on the cast-film line and then on a larger BOPET line
(Section~\ref{sec:reuse}); a scripted policy rather than a language model
issued the writes (two live engine turns follow). On the single-node
mission one write sufficed (\resMissionFrom\,$\rightarrow\,$\resMissionTo\,$^{\circ}$C,
settled at \resMissionPv\,$^{\circ}$C, journal-attributed, task
\texttt{COMPLETED}).
The BOPET mission is the multi-node case: nine devices and 49 signals
(\resBiaxSp{} writable, \resBiaxDaq{} readable), all ensure-pass created.
Three
governed writes on three distinct nodes---casting speed
$32\!\rightarrow\!33.8$\,m/min, fast-roll speed
$118\!\rightarrow\!120.3$\,m/min, and rail width $3000\!\rightarrow\!3035$\,mm---
bring thickness from \resBiaxStart{} to
\resBiaxFinal\,$\mu$m against
$\resBiaxTarget\pm\resBiaxTol$\,$\mu$m---$0.04$\,$\mu$m inside the window;
the same run's prose summary records it \emph{not} attained while its
machine-readable verdict records attained, so we report both. Each write opened a record,
received \texttt{keep}, and left an
agent-attributed journal entry, the parent task reaching \texttt{COMPLETED};
governed-write counts match accepted changes in both missions. Finally,
two live agent-engine turns (omp adapter, glm-5.3-flash)
drove the same slot on a scratch line---a bounded write
(\resLlmWall) and a goal-driven multi-step descent settling at
\resGoalFinal\,$\mu$m against a harness-supplied target ($52.0\pm0.8$\,$\mu$m
written into the task text, not the scenario predicate), saving a recipe with
attributed, audit-logged authorship (\resGoalWall). They show that an engine
can occupy the slot, not agent quality: the run's pass criterion is a
completion marker in the transcript.

\subsection{Q3: The Deterministic Closed Loop}
\label{sec:controller}
\looseness=-1 Seeds 42--44 each issue two governed writes with no rejection, raising the
cast-film objective $J$ from $J_{0}\approx\resJzero$ to
$J/J^{*}=\resControllerMin$--\resControllerMax{}, against
the best grid point $J^{*}=\resJstar$---an exhaustive grid search over the
admissible setpoints (zone temperature 195--225\,$^{\circ}$C in
2.5\,$^{\circ}$C steps, screw 50--200 in steps of five, line speed 20--120
in steps of 2.5\,m/min) on the same simulator, so $J^{*}$ is a lower
bound on the true optimum and the gap to unity is expected. What the benchmark
establishes is narrower: the governed path
passes a conventional controller's writes through unmodified---admissible,
journaled, read back. The claim is transparency of evidence handling,
not a performance delta.

\subsection{Q4: Four Process Missions}
\label{sec:scenarios}
\looseness=-1 The campaign runs four blueprint-provisioned lines concurrently; in this run
(seed 42) all four attain their archived predicates---evaluated from
subsequent observations, not task state. Fig.~\ref{fig:missions} archives
each mission's optimization trajectory.
Injection molding is the minimal case (two writes: hold pressure
45$\rightarrow$58.2\,bar, hold time 6$\rightarrow$10.64\,s), BOPET the multi-node case of
Section~\ref{sec:agentteam}; both close with task state and predicate agreeing, on separate
records. \looseness=-1 Wastewater treatment is the write-heaviest
mission (\resWwtpWrites{} governed writes): dissolved oxygen rises from
\resWwtpStart{} into $3.4\pm0.6$\,mg/L (attainment window; terminal
observation 3.50\,mg/L, Fig.~\ref{fig:missions}b) under five
effluent guards: four finish wider than at baseline, the
fifth, pH, drifting 6.59$\rightarrow$6.38. Its
most informative events are blower steps that were admissible and journaled
yet lost compliance margin in the post-write observation; the verdict stage
reverted them, so a bad proposal is rejected in the evidence, not at the
admission gate. Annealing starts far outside the hardness window
(\resAnnealStart\,HV at 140\,m/min); zone-3 temperature, one
zone-2 trim, and two
line-speed steps hold $95\pm6$\,HV while raising line speed by
\resAnnealGain, and a further attempt at \resAnnealSpeedTried\,m/min is
reverted for leaving the margin---on record. Mission clocks (\resInjWall,
\resWwtpWall, \resAnnealWall; BOPET's unarchived) are simulator
wall time under accelerated dynamics. Across the four missions in this run,
platform-side behavior is identical while the write economy---governed writes
per scripted policy---varies by more than a factor of seven (2, 15, 5, and 3).

\subsection{Q5: Deployment-Scale Regression and Write-Evidence Campaign}
\label{sec:hardening}
The final lens stresses the platform itself: the hardening pass made the
evidence grading, the line master switch, and the approval-anchored dispatch
interval of Section~\ref{sec:mech} the explicit test surface of the
closed-loop, fine-tuning, and write-evidence scenarios on the live instance
of Section~\ref{sec:testbed}.
\looseness=-1 \emph{Full-suite repeatability.} Three consecutive pipeline executions
complete with zero failed checks---79 checks per run on October 7--8
(237 total), 90--92 per run on October 9 (\resNovTotal{} total); two runs
land inside the per-line
trial cadence
(Section~\ref{sec:nodeexec}) and wait out the remaining \resCadenceWait---
pacing stays in force under automation.

\looseness=-1 \emph{Closed-loop scenario under governance.} Each run walks observation, grey-box-sensitized decision (dead-band
\resNovDeadband, slope \resNovSlope), a five-element per-parameter proposal,
HITL approval, batch dispatch, and three-way consistency (recipe, PLC
readback, mirror sample). The loop runs in two
regimes: October 7--8 descends $66\!\rightarrow\!63$\,bar one governed
step per run under converged dead-band excitation; October 9 answers a
sagging mirror weight (\resNovWeights) with a bounded zigzag
(\resNovZigzagFrom$\!\rightarrow\!$\resNovZigzagTo\,bar, converged) and
corrective steps (\resNovCorrFrom$\!\rightarrow\!$\resNovCorrTo\,bar).
The zigzag is a deliberate excitation, not a correction---the archived
decision records the loop already converged ($0.014$\,g against a
\resNovDeadband{} dead-band) and names exercising the dispatch chain as its
purpose---while the two corrective steps answer residual error
($0.169/0.276$\,g). Every step verifies
equal values across the three sources at maximum consistency error
\resConsistencyErr{} ($0.05$\,bar device, $0.51$\,bar bucketed-mirror
tolerances---all three legs equal exactly at the endpoint's recording
resolution, a passed tight check, not equality by
construction). The pacing window acts before
the human: in two runs the proposal is early-rejected by the trial cadence
and re-proposed \resNovCadenceReject{} later; only then is the HITL card
raised (scheme, proposer reasoning, per-parameter
basis) and structured approval releases the readback-verified dispatch
(Fig.~\ref{fig:loop}).
Approval-to-dispatch completes in \resHitlDispatchMs{};
observation-to-verification spans \resObsVerifyFast{} without a
pending cadence window (\resObsVerifyCadence{} with, October~7--8;
96.1\,s, October~9)---the loop's time is governance, not
platform overhead. The fine-tuning
scenario rides the same windows (Fig.~\ref{fig:loop}, lower lane),
and the diagnostics scenario exports a 30-min merged wide
table (\resRowsRange{} rows, 14 columns)---a feed data-quality
observation, not a process excursion.

\looseness=-1 Latency and mission figures are single-run point estimates
(about \resWriteNperLine{} writes per line, one trajectory each); only Q3 and Q5
are replicated.

\emph{Write-evidence legs.} A dedicated seven-leg suite verifies Sections~\ref{sec:ack}
and~\ref{sec:hitl} directly: the master switch refuses an unconfirmed manual-to-automatic
transition; a mock-acknowledged dispatch is promoted to readback-verified;
a broker cut marks affected parameters unverified while healthy siblings
verify; a publish-only endpoint accepts the write (broker puback,
\texttt{ok=true}) yet stays \emph{unverified} through three verification
attempts---the receipt Section~\ref{sec:ack}'s falsifiable rule is stated
against; and
on a manual-mode line an approval-free binding's dispatch is intercepted
into the queue, approval releasing all six parameters device-verified. An
API regression and a documentation-consistency suite close the campaign.

\looseness=-1 Across the five questions the pattern holds: admission intercepts and
attributes, grading degrades honestly, pacing holds under automation,
approval mediates without carrying the checks, and compensation and the
backstop recover on record---Section~\ref{sec:mech}'s mechanisms behave as
specified, with task state and attainment never merged into one number.
