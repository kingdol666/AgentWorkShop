\subsection{LLM Agents in Industrial Control: From Generation to Governance}
\looseness=-1 Agent-based manufacturing has long linked organizational roles to plant
capabilities \cite{leitao2009agentbased}; LLM-oriented industrial work now
spans PLC generation and verification \cite{liu2026agents4plc}. The closest
systems, characterized in Table~\ref{tab:nearest}, target the control action
itself: MADE couples multi-modal perception to control generation with a
safety layer and HITL verification, scoring its own task success, with no
persistent production-scoped write-evidence path reported
\cite{huang2026made}; K{\"o}hle et al.\ resolve tasks over a six-module
smart-factory plant of per-module LLM agents and tool servers---writes carry
module-scoped skills, with no versioned production context or write-evidence
chain reported \cite{koehle2026llmdriven}; Waseem et al.\ place pretrained
LLMs \emph{inside} the control loop as real-time controllers of a robotic
line \cite{waseem2026pretrained}---\system{} occupies a deliberately
different layer, above fast regulation, so the roles are complementary; Lim
and Kovalenko explore resource capabilities across manufacturing agents
\cite{lim2025llmenabled} and Zhao et al.\ dispatch shop-floor tasks
\cite{zhao2026shopfloor}---both allocate work among agents, whereas this
layer governs how an allocated proposal reaches the plant and what evidence
it leaves. A recent review frames agentic AI's industrial value as
safety-aware, explainable, HITL decision support rather than autonomous
control authority \cite{jiang2026agentic}; the contribution here is the
mechanism set and benchmark that make that positioning concrete, and
Table~\ref{tab:nearest} characterizes each system as reported in its
publication.

\subsection{Frameworks, Standards, Run-Time Guarantees, and Automation Practice}
Multi-agent frameworks coordinate tasks and tools, and the Model Context
Protocol (MCP) and Agent-to-Agent (A2A) specifications standardize tool and
inter-agent interfaces \cite{mcp2024,a2a2025}; the tool they expose, however,
is a bare function signature carrying no line, product, or recipe context,
with no record separating admission from execution. ISA-88 and ISA-95 supply
complementary models for recipes and operations
\cite{isa88,isa95}; \system{}'s versioned recipes and
run dispatch instantiate a reduced subset of that stack, and the governed
life cycle transposes run-to-run control disciplines \cite{moyne2000r2r}
onto a supervisory write path.
A separate line enforces run-time guarantees on LLM agents---judge and guard
agents, rule-based
enforcement \cite{xiang2024guardagent,debenedetti2025camel,wang2025agentspec};
they gate software tool calls, not plant writes, and their policies could
compile onto the enforcement substrate contributed here.

\looseness=-1 Permissions, range checks, approvals, and audit trails long existed in
distributed-control-system (DCS) and SCADA practice---versioned batch recipes and electronic batch journals in
batch-control systems---and IEC~62443 codifies their security counterpart \cite{iec62443-3-3},
while formal shielding, control-barrier functions, and simplex run-time
assurance offer stronger specified properties \cite{alshiekh2018shielding,ames2017cbf,sha2001simplex}.
Against that practice the differences are substantive: the proposer is a
machine-generated agent identity rather than a presupposed human operator,
so admission binds to member authority and prompt content stays inert, and is
re-evaluated per request against live product and recipe state through
versioned objects rather than at role-assignment time; the
governed path spans five exercised protocol families above any single control system;
and the evidence states---graded acknowledgment, three-way consistency,
verdict, compensation---survive strategy replacement, where the three-way
check is a supervised special case of classical data reconciliation and
gross-error detection \cite{crowe1996reconciliation}, applied across recipe,
device, and mirrored views of one setpoint rather than across plant balance
equations. Neither the six systems
above nor a classical chain provides that combination as a unit.
\system{} implements source-dependent checks and a rule-based backstop and
makes no formal protection guarantee, the backstop
(Section~\ref{sec:agentloop}) being a rule-based recovery action in the spirit
of ISA-18.2 and EEMUA-191 \cite{isa182,eemua191}; human oversight likewise
requires more than an approval dialog
\cite{bainbridge1983ironies}. Agent benchmarks
\cite{liu2024agentbench} and prompt-injection studies
\cite{greshake2023injection,liu2024pinjection} inform adjacent evaluations;
ours separates source checks, protocol execution, mechanism ablation,
controller trials, and multi-scenario missions. In sum: coordination frameworks supply no production context, standards no
execution path, guard agents gate software tools, and nearest systems stop at
the control action---the open slot is the governed, plant-facing write path
itself.
