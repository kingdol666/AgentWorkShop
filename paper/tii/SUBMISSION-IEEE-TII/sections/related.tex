\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
\subsection{Industrial Agents and Closed-Loop Applications}
Agent-based manufacturing has long connected organizational roles to industrial
capabilities \cite{leitao2009agentbased}. Recent LLM-oriented work spans
industrial knowledge graphs \cite{zhang2026comaikg}, diagnosis
\cite{liu2024kgdiag}, PLC generation and verification
\cite{liu2026agents4plc,fakih2024llm4plc}, digital-twin configuration
\cite{xia2024llmsim}, OPC UA machine control \cite{hofmann2025nlc}, and
simulated fault handling \cite{vyas2026ftc,gill2025llmdt}, while InstructMPC
combines language-derived objectives with model-predictive control
\cite{wu2025instructmpc}. These studies motivate agent-facing industrial tools;
the shared execution layer carrying authority and evidence across
scenarios---our focus---is complementary rather than a new
controller or reasoning method.

\subsection{Orchestration, Industrial Models, and Governance}
AutoGen, LangGraph, and CrewAI coordinate tasks and tools
\cite{wu2023autogen,langgraph2024,crewai2024}, and the Model Context Protocol
(MCP) and Agent-to-Agent (A2A) specifications standardize tool and inter-agent
interfaces \cite{mcp2024,a2a2025}. These frameworks coordinate reasoning and
tool invocation, but the tool they expose is usually a bare function signature
carrying no line, product, or recipe context, with no record separating
admission from execution. ISA-88, ISA-95, ISO~23247, and IEC~63278 supply
complementary models for recipes, operations, and digital twins
\cite{isa88,isa95,iso23247,iec63278}, and \system{}'s versioned recipes and run
dispatch instantiate a reduced subset of that stack: master-recipe-style
versions over a line--product--run resource model, without batch procedure
models or a full equipment hierarchy. The governed life cycle itself---bounded
proposals, post-write holds, per-run journals---transposes the disciplines of
run-to-run control \cite{moyne2000r2r} onto a supervisory write path; the
increment here is interchangeable strategies over heterogeneous protocols with
evidence that survives strategy replacement.
Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) and shop-floor agent architectures likewise emphasize contextualized
integration \cite{gautam2025iiot}, and the nearest neighbors
\cite{biskupovic2025supervision,zhao2026shopfloor} target control decoupling
and task decomposition. A recent line instead enforces runtime guarantees on
LLM agents---judge and guard agents, capability-based information flow,
rule-based enforcement
\cite{xiang2024guardagent,debenedetti2025camel,wang2025agentspec}; these gate
software tool calls; none owns a physical write path with device readback and
a write journal; and their policies could compile onto the enforcement
substrate contributed here, whose differentiator is production-scoped
authority and evidence.

Permissions, range checks, approvals, and audit trails have DCS/SCADA/MES and
IEC~62443 counterparts \cite{iec62443-3-3}, while formal shielding,
control-barrier functions, and simplex run-time assurance offer stronger
specified properties \cite{alshiekh2018shielding,ames2017cbf,sha2001simplex}.
\system{} instead implements source-dependent checks and a rule-based backstop
and makes no formal protection guarantee: the backstop
(Section~\ref{sec:agentloop}) is a rule-based recovery action in the spirit of
ISA-18.2 and EEMUA-191 \cite{isa182,eemua191} rather than a run-time assurance
bound. Human oversight likewise requires more than an approval dialog
\cite{parasuraman2000model,bainbridge1983ironies}. Agent benchmarks
\cite{liu2024agentbench,yao2024taubench,xu2024company}, process-monitoring
datasets \cite{downs1993tep,goh2017swat}, and prompt-injection studies
\cite{greshake2023injection,liu2024pinjection} inform adjacent evaluations; ours
separates source checks, protocol execution, mechanism ablation,
controller trials, and multi-scenario missions.
