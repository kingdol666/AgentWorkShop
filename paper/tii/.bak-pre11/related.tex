\section{Related Work}
\subsection{Industrial Agents and Closed-Loop Applications}
Agent-based distributed manufacturing control has a two-decade history
\cite{leitao2009agentbased}; what is contributed here is the binding of LLM-team
membership to node capability and run context. Industrial LLM applications span
industrial knowledge-graph construction \cite{zhang2026comaikg} and diagnosis
\cite{liu2024kgdiag}, PLC program generation and verification
\cite{liu2026agents4plc,fakih2024llm4plc}, and digital-twin configuration
\cite{xia2024llmsim}. Not all industrial agents are advisory: natural-language interfaces have controlled machines over OPC UA \cite{hofmann2025nlc}, LLM
agents have been studied in simulated fault-tolerant control \cite{vyas2026ftc},
and InstructMPC uses language-derived context for disturbance prediction in
model-predictive control \cite{wu2025instructmpc}. Closest to our setting,
agent-based supervision has been studied for service-oriented industrial
cyber-physical systems \cite{biskupovic2025supervision}, and LLM-based multi-agent
systems coordinate shopfloor manufacturing tasks \cite{zhao2026shopfloor}; neither
binds individual team members to specific nodes with source-dependent execution
checks and line/product/recipe/run attribution. Our focus is that reusable binding,
with the governed write path and optimization records it enables, not a new control
law or CPS middleware.

\subsection{Orchestration, Industrial Models, and Execution Governance}
AutoGen, LangGraph, and CrewAI organize agent tasks and tool use
\cite{wu2023autogen,langgraph2024,crewai2024}, and MCP and A2A standardize tool and
inter-agent interfaces \cite{mcp2024,a2a2025}; industrial integration additionally
requires node semantics, driver behavior, and operating context. We use a
line/product/recipe/run organization informed by ISA-95 and ISA-88
\cite{isa95,isa88}: line and product correspond to level-3 production work units and
product definitions, run to production-performance records, and recipe to the ISA-88
master-recipe idea with grade and operating-point semantics on continuous lines;
we do not implement the complete procedural
models of either standard. The device-twin terminology presumes manufacturing
digital twins whose assets carry structured semantic descriptions
\cite{nee2019digital}, and the node semantic card is analogous to an Asset
Administration Shell submodel \cite{iec63278}. A goal-driven unit's
observe--hypothesize--write--re-observe cycle likewise resembles autonomous chemical
research with LLMs \cite{boiko2023autonomous}, which closes the loop over physical
experiments; our writes are governed industrial setpoints rather than self-directed
experiment planning.

The ingredients of a governed write---node-scoped permission, range validation,
approval, and an audit trail---have counterparts in mature DCS/SCADA/MES practice,
typically enforced as role- and application-level controls under IEC~62443-3-3
\cite{iec62443-3-3}; supervisory optimization layers add output clamping,
write-enable, and fallback to base control. LLM-mediated writes additionally require
member-instance-level binding, semantic linkage between a write and the verdict or
optimization record that produced it, and attribution across engine boundaries. Hard action shielding
and control-barrier methods enforce formally specified admissible actions
\cite{alshiekh2018shielding}; the rollback manager and rule-based backstop are
engineering instances of run-time assurance and simplex-style architectures
\cite{sha2001simplex}, and are narrower implementation-level checks rather than
substitutes for certified plant protection. Human supervision also requires a clear
division of responsibility, not merely an approval dialog
\cite{parasuraman2000model,bainbridge1983ironies}.

\subsection{Evaluation of Tools and Industrial Control}
AgentBench and consequential tool-use benchmarks evaluate agent behavior and policy
compliance \cite{liu2024agentbench,yao2024taubench}. Industrial datasets such as TEP
and SWaT support process-monitoring research \cite{downs1993tep,goh2017swat}, but
replay is not evidence of successful physical actuation, so we separate source
checks, mock-driver probes, software-protocol integration, fixed-controller
optimization, and exploratory LLM runs. The threat model of
indirect prompt injection \cite{greshake2023injection}---a compromised agent writing
through legitimate tools---is what the write-side checks are designed to contain,
although our evaluation includes no adversarial LLM probes.
